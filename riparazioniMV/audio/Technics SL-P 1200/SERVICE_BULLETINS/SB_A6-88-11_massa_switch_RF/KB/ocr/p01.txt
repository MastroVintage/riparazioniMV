Symptoms: 1) A popping noise is heard through the Audio output
when PLAY initiates.

2) The PLAY function is accidently interrupted; the
pick-up remains at the current location or resets
to its rest position. In either case erratic
operation is noticed.

3) The unit does not respond to the wired remote
control PLAY command.

4) The unit does not read the Disc’s TOC or skips
occasionally.

NOTE:
MAKE COPIES OF THIS SERVICE BULLETIN
FOR THE FOLLOWING MODELS AND FILE IT

WITH THE SERVICE MANUAL:
SL-P1200 & SL-P1200X

Causes: 1) Improper grounding between this unit and the rest
of the systen.

2) The control panel is not properly grounded, causing
static electricity to affect normal operation.

3) Switch bouncing causes the unit to misinterpret the
PLAY key function.

4) Dust accumulates on the optical pick-up lens. The
OPEN/CLOSE detection switch may be defective.

Remedies: Perform the following modifications. Note that all of
the following parts (except the lens cleaning Kit
SZZP1038C) are supplied in a kit as indicated on page 3.

1) Mount a ground lug according to the following:
A. Drill a 4mm hole on the indicated location of the
rear panel. Make sure that no filings fall inside
the unit.
