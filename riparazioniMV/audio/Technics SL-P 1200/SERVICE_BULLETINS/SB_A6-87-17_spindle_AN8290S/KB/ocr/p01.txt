Symptom: 1) No operation at all.

2) Unit reads the T.0.C. but fails to enter the play
mode.

SL-P1200
Compact Disc Player

$L-P1200

Cause: The spindle drive IC (AN8290S) overheats and locks-up
when:

A) The unit is subjected to excessive vibration during
play causing the pick-up to skip.

B) The unit is subjected to excessive use of the skip
function.

C) The play function is selected while the transport
lock shaft is not set free.

Each of the above conditions cause Q@12, Q@13, Q101, and
Q102 to overheat and become damaged. The excessive heat
causes the transistor’s solder joints to dry and
therefore cause poor connection, or solder bridges.

Remedy: 1) Order kit number SZZP1093C. Refer to the kit contents
below.
2) Replace the transistors as indicated in Figure 1.
3) Mount the heat sink to IC501 as shown and indicated
in Figure 2.
