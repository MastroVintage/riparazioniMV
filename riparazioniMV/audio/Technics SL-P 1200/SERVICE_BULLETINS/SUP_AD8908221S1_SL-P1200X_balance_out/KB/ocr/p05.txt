IMPORTANT!

This SL-P1200 or SL-P1200X Compact Disc Player has been modified by improving
internal ground circuit for keyboard. An external ground terminal has also
been added. PLEASE FOLLOW THESE INSTRUCTIONS WHEN INSTALLING so as to prevent
static or mis-operation, especially when using wired remote. Thank you.
