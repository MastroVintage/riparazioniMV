Symptom: Playability deteriorates when playing 8cm discs, especially at the outer
periphery.
Cause: - The disc magnet assembly (ref. #4 in the Service Manual) does not

secure the disc to the spindle effectively, causing the disc to slip and vibrate.

Remedy: Change the pressure rubber mounted to the bottom of the disc magnet
assem sly (ref. #4 in the Service Manual). The thickness of the original
pressure rubber isO0.5mm. The new one (Part Number SHGD155-1) is

ww 2.5 +/-0.3mm thick.

1) Remove the original pressure rubber making sure no adhesive residues
remain on the magnet assembly.

2) Remove the protective paper from the new pressure rubber. Mount the
pressure rubber to the disc magnet assembly.
