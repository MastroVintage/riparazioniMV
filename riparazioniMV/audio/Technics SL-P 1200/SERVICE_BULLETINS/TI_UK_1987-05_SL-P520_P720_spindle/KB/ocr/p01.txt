MODELS: SL-P520, SL-P720
SYMPTOMS: No operations or the unit reads TOC but fails to enter into play mode.

CAUSE: When spindle drive IC is overheated and locked up while subjected to
excessive vibration; excessive use of cue/review, and set switched on
and put in play with transit clip left on; IC4 AND IC5 overheats and ~
internal IC fuses go open.

Thermal runaway also causes transistors Q11, Q12, Q101 and Q102 to go
short, and heat often causes these transistor legs solder to give dry
joint.

CURE: Fit the components in Kit SZZP1090C.
METHOD: (1) Replace Q11 and Q101 by 2SC1846 ©

Ql2 and Q102 by 25A885
