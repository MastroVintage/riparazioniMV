7. Timer Play

1. Turn on the power switches of all components in the
timer play chain.

Note: The timer of the audio timer should be off at this

time.
2. Set the amplifier input selector to the position for this
player.

. Insert a disc in the holder.

4. Confirm the present time and set the audio timer to
the desired start and stop times.

5. Turn the audio timer on. The audio timer will now turn
power off to all components in the timer play chain.

6. The disc will now be played from track one at the
preset time.
