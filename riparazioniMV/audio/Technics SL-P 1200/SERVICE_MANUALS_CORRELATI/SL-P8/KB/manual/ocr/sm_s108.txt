13. Amp. Gain Adjustment

(Visual check)

1) Push the power switch “ON” of the set.

2) When push any key of the transmitter,and confirm a sensor LED on the front panel of the set to
light. and then keep a distance about 7m between transmitter and receiver in a straight line.

3) And confirm a sensor LED whether it lights or is doesnt.
If it doesnt light adjust R1103,so that the sensor LED will be lighted.
