FOREWORD

This trouble shooting is intended to be used for seminars and selfstudies in Combination with the
trouble shooting of SL-7. Therefore those Common to SL-P7 are omitted.

Accordingly, main differences from SL-P7 are involved.
1. Remote Control system and Expanded function of Operation Control.
2. Pitch Control.
3. Expansion of Display function.

These trouble shooting are described in 3 chapters, and other related circuit are omitted, So
refer to the trouble shooting of SL-P7 and adjustment manual of SL-P8.
