Fig. 20 Display circuit

FL lighting operation

Display digit is decided from C output port of display
microcomputer, and 7-segment display data is delivered

from D port for the purpose of track and time display.

The music No. bar code is lighted by E00 - E03, C07 and
DO7. The output of DO7 becomes the "over" mark command
(over 21 tunes) during the 4th digit display, and the colon
lighting command of segment display during the 5th digit
display.

Also, 4 LED's are lighted by the output of C08 - COll.
