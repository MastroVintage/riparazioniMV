*Outline of FL ON operation
Display digits are determined by C output ports (C01
— C06); segment data are put out from D ports (DOO —
DO6); and bar codes are lighted by E00 — E03, CO7,
DO7.
DO7 port output becomes over ( ®) lighting assignment
output during the 4th digits display, and colon (:)
lighting assignment output for segment display during
the 5th digit display.
The 4 LED’s (play, pause/stop, repeat, music scan) are
lighted by CO8 — CO11 outputs. :

controll (IMMN1564)

Entry of ‘‘DSEND” signal from MN1564 into SNS1
terminal results in display data receiving. Display data
from A input port is received while ‘““‘DCLOCK”’ signal
that enters SNSO terminal from MN1564 is checked.
With data received, it delivers ‘“‘DECHO” signal from COO
to MN1564 and waits for the next receiving timing.
