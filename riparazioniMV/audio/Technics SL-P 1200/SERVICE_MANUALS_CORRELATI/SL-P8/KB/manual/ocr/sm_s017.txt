How to remove the loading motor

Remove the head amplifier P.C.B.

Remove the belt from the loading motor pulley.

Remove the 2 setscrews [Fig. 17: @ , @ 1] of loading
motor, and unsolder the motor leads.

*Connect the brown lead to the (+) terminal of motor,
and red lead to (—) terminal. (The (—) terminal is indi-
cated by arrow on the motor.)
