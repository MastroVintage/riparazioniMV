Configuration of the circuit

Microcomputer for remote-control decode is inserted between
OP CON and key matrix so that only outputs with various
switches of key matrix turned ON are put in P00 - P03 and P10 of
OP CON.

Similarly, REMO CON signal is put in OP CON through micro-
computer for remote-control decode.
