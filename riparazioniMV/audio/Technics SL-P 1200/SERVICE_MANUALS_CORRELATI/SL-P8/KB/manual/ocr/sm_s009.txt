. Press the number 5 of the numeric button.
. Press the time button.

. Press the time button.
. Press the number 3 of the numeric button.
. Press the number 8 of the numeric button.
. Press the play button.
. Disc play begins from the 2 min, 38s. of track 5.

to the first track and the player switches to the standby
