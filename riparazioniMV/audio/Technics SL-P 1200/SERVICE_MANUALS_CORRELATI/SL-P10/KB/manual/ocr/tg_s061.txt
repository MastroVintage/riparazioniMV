7. RF data pick-out circuit

7-1. Roles of RF data pick-out circuit

This circuit is located in the middle of optical PU and digi-
tal processing circuit, and takes two different roles.

The first role is RF signal waveform shaping. The signal
recorded on the disc is digital signal of 4.32 MHz bit rate,
and the output waveform of optical PU obtained through repro-
duction is continuous analod signal as shown in Pig. 7-1.
Therefore, it is necessary to shape the waveform into the form
of digital signal. The second role is to generate PCK (clock
Signal). PCK is a bit rate signal synchronizing with the
Signal reproduced from the disc.

Fig. 7—1

The signal reproduced from the disc has some jitter until be-
ing stored into the memory. Therefore, clock Signal synchro-
nizing with the reproduced signal is required so that the
digital processor is able to correctly receive the data. PCK
is made by use of PLL circuit.
