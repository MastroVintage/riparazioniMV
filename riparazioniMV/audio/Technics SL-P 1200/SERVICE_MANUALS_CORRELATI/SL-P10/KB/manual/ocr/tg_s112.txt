Door open/close is done by micro-switch in key matrix.

Door is closed with disc inserted or closed key depress-—
ed. LD ON and TT ON are delivered when both DISC ON

and LMT ON sensor signals are received by system micro-
computer.

With traverse step forward and LMT OFF sensor signal
confirmed, then traverse steps and FO ON is delivered.

FO ON causes focus search, and with RF ON sensor signal
confirmed, TR ON is delivered, shifting traverse to play
mode.

Servo is completed with TR ON, then TOC is read and
displayed.

After TOC reading, LD/TT ON, FO ON and TR ON are OFF.
During TOC reading, only PLAY key is accepted.

* Pause repeat key is also accepted but it is released
