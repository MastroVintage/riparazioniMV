e Function of optical servo circuit

Unlike the playback of conventional analog disc, the player
employs a system using a laser pick-up to take out the signal.

Focus servo, tracking servo and traverse servo circuits are
necessary for optical servo, and each individual servo function
is explained in the following.

e Focus servo

Focus servo moves the focus lens at right angles to the disc so
that the laser beam is always focused to the pit of the disc.

Tracking servo moves the focus lens horizontally to the disc so
that the laser beam always follows the track of the disc.

The purpose is to align the focus of laser beam to the disc
pit in order to correctly pick up disc information by laser
beam. Also, disc is deflected (in the direction of focus)

and is permitted up to 300 um by the disc standard. Accord-
ingly, if the deflection in the direction of focus is within

+300 um, then the laser beam will be properly focused to the
disc pit.

e Tracking servo

Tracking servo moves the focus lens horizontally to the disc so that the laser beam always
the track of the disc.

The purpose is to let the laser beam follow the track from
inner to outer periphery of disc in order to correctly read
the information from disc without sound grooves like analog
record, Also, disc is deviated (in the tracking direction)
and is nermitted up to +70 um by the disc standard. Accord-
ingly, if the deflection in the tracking direction is within
+70 um, then the laser beam will correctly follow the disc
track.

e Traververse servo

The stepping motor is rotated step by step to move the optical
pickup toward :the inner or outer periphery of the disc. The
track following distance is limited to the range in which the
focus lens is movable since the tracking servo follows the disc
track while moving the focus lens. The distance is 200 um max.
and this is not enough to let the laser beam follow all the track
recorded on the disc with a radius of 60 um. Accordingly, when
the focus lens has followed the track by about 62.5 um from the
tracking servo, the stepping motor is rotated by 1 step to move
the optical pickup in the focus lens by 62.5 um. In this way,
the range of movable focus lens is expanded so that the laser
beam can follow all the track on the disc.
