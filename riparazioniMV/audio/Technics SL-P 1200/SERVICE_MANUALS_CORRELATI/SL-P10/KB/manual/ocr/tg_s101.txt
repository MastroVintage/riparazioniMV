Data transfer from MN66ll to system microcomputer

The data transferred from MN661l to system microcomputer are
control Q data demodulated from EFM data.

The control Q data are not directly transferred but they are
once written into external RAM IC706 (MN2114) and are read by
system microcomputer as needed.

(Both data and address lines are bus line system.) The data
transfer between MN6611 -- external RAM -- system microcompu-
