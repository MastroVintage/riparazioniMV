When the circuit receives LD ON signal from the system micro-
computer, the level at pin (4) of IC252 becomes "H".

Then, the level at pin (8) becomes "H" causing Q251 to operate
and LD (laser diode) lights up. At the same time, LD STATE
(laser confirming) signal at pin (6) becomes "H".,

APC operation detects the light intensity of LD by the pin
diode.

If the light intensity of LD is increased, the voltage at pin
(7) is lowered, causing the voltage at pin (8) to lower.
Conversely, if the light intensity of LD is decreased, the
voltage is lowered.

° VR254 is a semi-fixed resistor to change the voltage detected

at pin (7) of I1C252, and it is able to change the light
intensity of LD. In SL-P10, it is adjusted so that the
light from the focus lens is 0.32 mW.

Incidentally, -15 V from CN251 pin (9) is supplied through LD
switch (that turns ON with holder closed) of the loading mecha-
nism.
