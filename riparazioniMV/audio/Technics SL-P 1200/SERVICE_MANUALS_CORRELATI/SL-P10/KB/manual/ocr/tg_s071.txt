Drop-out control circuit

IC603 (MN4066B) pins (1) - (2) and (3) - (4) are drop-out
control circuit. If RF signal is missing due to drop-out,
then 0.5T edge pulse cannot be detected and PLL does not
operate. So, in case of drop-out, it is remedied through
the following operation.

Drop-out detection signal ("H" in drop-out mode) is applied

to CN605 pin (1). (Refer to the section of Drop-out detection
circuit on page 68. ) Thus, during normal playback mode, RF
signal with shaped waveform is selected. T-EFM signal is
selected in drop-out mode.

T-EFM signal is pure electrical EFM data mode by demodulation
LSI.

That is, during drop-out, PCK is properly controlled through
substitution with T.EFM, while the correcting circuit of
demodulation LSI will take care of data missing.
