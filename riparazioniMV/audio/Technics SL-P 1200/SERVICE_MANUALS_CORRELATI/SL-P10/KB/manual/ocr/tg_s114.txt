After TOC reading, either of manual play and program play
can be used. (Refer to Fig. 11-17.)

— Manual play —

Play is started in order from the lst tune, and is completed
on detection of the last read-out.

The play position is designated by program key, and the
program is played through random access.

Program reception is possible up to 63 steps. In the
case of group program, 2 steps (designation of start and
end positions) are included in 1 program.

When the commands are filled up, then "F" is displayed.
And "E" is displayed where there is no designation on
the disc.

When program key (10-key) is depressed during play mode,
the mode is shifted to program reception mode. In this
condition, if key is depressed with 1 step of
program completed, then all the previous programs are
cleared resulting in entry of new programs.

If program key is not depressed for consecutive 25 sec.
or over during program or if PLAY key is not depressed
within 25 sec. after the end of program, the program
will be cancelled and the mode is shifted to manual
play mode.
