5. Traverse servo circuit

5—1. Function of traverse servo

Traverse servo functions to move the whole of optical PU
from inner to outer and outer to inner periphery.

The tracking servo moves the object lens in the direction
of tracking to follow the track, while the travese servo
covers the radius of disc.

That is, tracking servo operates on the extended line of
tracking servo, and moves the traverse base (whole of ovti-
cal PU) when the lens is inclined by a specific angle. Thus,
the lens always follows the track of disc within the range
of tracking servo.
