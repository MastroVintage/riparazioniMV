11—3. Operation control circuit operation

Key matrix circuit

The configuration of key matrix is shown in Fig. 11-2. The
key matrix includes 6 scan outputs (P12, P13 and P60 - P63)
and 4 key inputs (P70 - P73).

OPEN/SHUT is the microswitch in the loading mechanism that
determines whether door is open or closed.

All the others are the keys on the front panel. HI is a
2-step key of forward (a) and reverse (@<@) of Search and
it is ON when the key is depressed to high speed mode.

* In high-speed mode of search (> Pb) , both FWD and HI are
ON. in low-speed mode, only FWD is ON.
