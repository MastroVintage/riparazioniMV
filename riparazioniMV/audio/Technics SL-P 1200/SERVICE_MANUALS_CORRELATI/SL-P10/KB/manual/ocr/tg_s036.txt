(3) crosses with V REF (reference voltage) of IC103 pin (7),
then IC103 pin (20) goes "H",.

Consequently, FG signal applied to IC909 pin (8) is deliver-
ed to 1¢909 pin (10). FG signal thus delivered causes the
system microcomputer to be informed of the ZERO CROSS point.
