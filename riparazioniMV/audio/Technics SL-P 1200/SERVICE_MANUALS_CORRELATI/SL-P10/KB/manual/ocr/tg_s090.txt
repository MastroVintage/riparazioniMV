Fig. 10-6. L.P.F Buffer circuit

The signal output from deglitch circuit is of stepped
waveform as in Fig. 10-7, therefore it is attenuated by
over 20 kHz by the low pass filter circuit consisting of
LPF801 and IC811 and is changed back to the original
recorded audio signal as in Fig. 10-8 and is delivered

to LINE OUT after impedance change by IC807.

IC807 (analog switch), C815 and R815 set up a de-emphasis
circuit that is controlled with the emphasis signal of
system microcomputer (MN1564). When the emphasis control
data contained in Q channel of the disk is "0001", CN702
pin (4) goes "H" to de-emphasize the output signal deliver-
ed_to—IC811. pin (Jo).

RL851 (relay), Q851 and 853 set up a muting circuit that
is controlled with the mute signal of system microcomputer
and DLY+5V. DLY+5V becomes +5 V about 140 ms after power
switch ON. Mute signal is at "L" in play mode but at "H"
in other modes, turning RL851 (relay) OFF.
