Fig. 10—5. Deglitch timing chart

PAM signal from D/A converter circuit is separated into
Lch and Rch signal by the switching circuit consisting of
Ic802, 805, 806 and Sl - S4 (1C813, 814).

The separation mechanism is explained in the following.
Suppose the PAM signal input in the circuit of Fic.10-4

is at point (A) of Fig. 10-5 in terms of time axis, then
Ic802 pin (5) is at "H" and pin (6) at "L", therefore Sl
turns ON and S2 OFF. Accordingly, the signal at poin A

of input PAM signal passes through Sl and is delivered to
Ic805 pin (1) (Lech OUT). In this case, S3 is OFF and S4
is ON, therefore the input PAM signal is not delivered

to I1C805 pin (1) (Rch OUT), and the previous state (signal
at point C) is held by C812. Next, suppose the PAM signal
is at point B in terms of time axis, then the operations
of Sl - S4 at point A are reversed. So, the signal at B
of PAM signal is delivered to IC806 pin (1) (Rch OUT),
while IC805 pin (1) (Lch OUT) holds the previous state
(signal at point A).
