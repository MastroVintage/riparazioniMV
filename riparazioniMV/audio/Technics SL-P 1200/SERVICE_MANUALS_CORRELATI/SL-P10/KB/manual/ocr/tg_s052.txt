The mode command signal from system microcomputer is
applied to B10, Bll, and Bl2 and "ZL" during stop and
pause modes.

IC1206 operates receiving these signals as input. There-
fore, the gate output in the above-mentioned mode is at
"H" and plays the same role as FCN Signal.
