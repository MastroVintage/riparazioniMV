TE and FE signals passed through servo loop switches S; and S>
are amplified by TE AMP and FE AMP, and then they are convert-
ed into (TE + FE) signal and (TE - FE) signal before being
delivered to IC101 pin (11) and pin (12).

Signals delivered to IC101 pins (11), (12) are applied to
IC105 and IC106 pin (1) where they are amplified in power,
and work to drive the actuator coil.
