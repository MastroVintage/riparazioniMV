Drop-out detection circuit

detection.

IC901 applies RF signal to pin (2) (plus input), and the
signal (RF level converted to DC) to pin (3) (minus input)
for the purpose of comparison. It is illustrated in Fig. 8-2.

The drop-out is generated when the input voltage is in the
relation of (-) > (4).
