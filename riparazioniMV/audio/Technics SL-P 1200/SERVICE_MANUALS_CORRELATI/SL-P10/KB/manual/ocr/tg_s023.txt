(1) Tracking signals detected by tracking photo detector
B,; and Bs are amplified by IC201 (A) and IC202 (A).
The signals are then converted to positive and nega-
tive tracking signals by the detection circuit consist-
ing of IC201 (B), 202 (B), and D201 ~ 204,

(2) The positive and negative signals are added at R213

(3) The tracking error signal is amplified at IC203 (A),
and is delivered to the low frequency compensating
circuit in the next stage. TE signal controls the
traverse servo.
