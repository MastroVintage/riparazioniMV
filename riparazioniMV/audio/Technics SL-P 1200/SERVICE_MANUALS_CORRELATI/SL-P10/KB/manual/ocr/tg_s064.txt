IC609 (745124) is VOC of PLL, and the frequency of output pin
(10) changes according to the DC voltage of pin (1) (FC input).
The free-run frequency of VCO is about 17.28 MHz. This output
is subjected to 1/4 frequency division at IC606, changing to
PCK of about 4.32 MHz (= 1T).

Ic605 (A) is PC (phase comparator) and are controlled by PCK
(= 1T) and edge pulse (= 0.5T). It is illustrated in Fig. 7-6.
PLL is locked when the phase difference of PCK and 0.5T is 90°.
