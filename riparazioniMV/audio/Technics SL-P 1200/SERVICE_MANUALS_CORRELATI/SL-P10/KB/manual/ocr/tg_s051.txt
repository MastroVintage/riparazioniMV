pulse waveforms from traverse microcomputer (MN1400)

core is reversed.

-At input terminal of AN7676, the 4-phase drive pulse wave-
form is made trapezoidal by C132 - C135.

(09104 is OFF during PLAY and manual search low-speed mode.)

During FWD mode, drive pulse changes as 9>3+6+C>+ 9

During REV mode, it changes as9*>Cr+67+32+9...
MN1400 pin (3) (C08) output makes AN7676 full power to
increase the starting torque.

Therefore, the stepping motor repeats Start + Hold opera-
tion.

Also, pin (2) (C09) goes "H" only when the traverse mode
moves at extra-high sveed, and changes the drive pulse
from trapezoidal wave to rectangular wave thereby releas-
ing the slow up motion of stepping motor.
