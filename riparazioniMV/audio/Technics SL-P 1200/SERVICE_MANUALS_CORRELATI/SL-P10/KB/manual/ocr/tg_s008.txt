2. Optical pick-up and traverse unit

2—1. Function of optical pickup

Optical pickup. (hereinafter called "optical PU") corresponds
to the cartridge of conventional analog player. It employs
a non-contact optical system and has two functions.

One is to pick up the signal (RF signal in CD) recorded on

a disk the same as in cartridge.

Another is an important function to detect focus error and
tracking error, thereby providing servo function.
