Suppose that the rotor magnet and stator core are as shown
in Fig. 5-8.

Then in tl timing of 4-phase pulse, exciting current "ic"
and "iD" flows into stator coils gC and gD. Suppose that
stator coils (Al) and (Bl) are excited in S pole due to
ic and iD, then the rotor magnet rotates 45° clockwise.

Next, iD and iA flow in t2 timing. S pole remains at (B1)
due to iD, but N pole is excited at (Al) due to iA.

In other words, S pole is excited at (A2). Accordingly,
the rotor magnet rotates 45° clockwise in t2 timing.
Current iA and iB flow in t3 timing.

S pole remains at (A2) due to iA, and S pole is excited
at (B2) due to iB, and further the rotor magnet rotates
45° in the same direction. (In this timing, Mgl comes to
the position of Mg2.)

Similarly, the rotor magnet rotates 45° in t4 timing, and
after that, it repeats rotating 45° each time the drive
pulse changes, and the motor makes one turn in 8 steps.
