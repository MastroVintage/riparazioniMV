Check of skip search

1) Play an ordinary disc.

2) Press the skip button and check to see that skip
search is given (Forward and reverse)

Check of manual search

1) Play an ordinary disc.

2) Press the manual search button and check to see
that smooth manual search can be done at low and
high speeds (Forward and reverse)

Check to make sure the best adjusted condition for

defects.

1) Play the Technics test disc "SZZP1054C".

2) Play the track 12 (Wedge 0.6 mm), track 13 (Wedge
0.7mm), and see that there is no sound skip or
noise.

3) Play the tracks (Black spot 0.6mm), track 6 (Black
spot 0.7mm) and see that there is no sound skip or
noise.
