How to remove the turntable

. Remove the traverse unit.
. Remove the spindle motor drive coil P.C.B.
. Remove the C ring which fastens the magnet shaft of

spindle motor, [Fig. 22]

. Remove the screw [Fig. 22: @)] by use of a hexagonal

wrench, then the turntable can be removed.

*If the turntable is removed, it is necessary to re-adjust
the height of turntable.

How to remove the laser pickup

. Remove the traverse unit.
. Remove the optical servo P.C.B.
. Pull out the connectors (CN101, CN102) of optical

servo P.C.B.

. Remove the bracket setscrew [Fig. 21: @)] of pickup

to remove the bracket.
Remove the guide rail retainer setscrew [Fig. 22 : @ |
and pull out the laser pickup from the two guide rails.

*The pickup must be re-adjusted when it is replaced or
fitted after removal.

How to remove the bottom cover of remote
control unit

. Remove the battery cover in the direction of the arrow

and take out the batteries.

. Remove the bottom cover setscrew. (Fig. 23: @)
. Insert a screwdriver between bottom and top covers to

release them little by little.

. Releasing the periphery of cover in a similar manner,

remove the bottom cover. (Portions 1 ~ 4)
