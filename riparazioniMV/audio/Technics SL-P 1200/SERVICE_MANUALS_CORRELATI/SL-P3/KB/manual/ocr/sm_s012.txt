Precuations for disc clamper fitting

When fitting the disc clamper again, follow the pro-

cedure given below. (Be sure to turn power switch

“off".)

1, Remove the magnet of disc clamper.

2. Temporarily tighten the 4 setscrews of disc clamper
with disc holder projected.

3. Close the disc holder.

4. Move the disc clamper so that the size between the
turntable platter center spindle and disc clamper is
as shown below. Then tighten the disc clamper
setscrews.

5. Set the magnet in place.

Note: If the above-mentioned size is deflected, noise

might be produced from disc clamper during

play mode.
