4, Tracking gain adjustment

1. Set the servo gain adjuster power SW “on” and the
rotary selector switch to “3°,

2. Adjust the low frequency oscillator to 1.5 kHz and
150mVp-p output. And connect it to TEST pin OSC
IN and GND of servo gain adjuster.

3. Connect CH1 and CH2 of oscilloscope to TEST pin

TP1 and TP2 of the servo gain adjuster. (TP3 is GND),

and place the disc on turntable.

Oscilloscope setting: VOLT 100mV (both channels),

SWEEP 2ms, Input AC, Mode

CHOP TP2
4. Turn power switch ON. When turntable beings to rotate,

1.5 kHz signal appears on the oscilloscope. Then adjust
VR104 so that the waveform amplitudes of both channels,
are equal to each other.

6. Tracking balance adjustment

1. Set the servo gain adjuster power SW “on” and the
rotary selector switch at “2”.

2. Connect the oscilloscope to TJ103 on 03 P.C.B. and
then place the disc on turntable.
Oscilloscope setting:

VOLT 200mV, SWEEP 5ms, input DC

3. Turn power switch ON. When turntable begins to rotate
and the tracking servo switch of servo gain adjuster is
turned OFF, TE (tracking error) signal appears, on the
ascilloscope.
Then adjust VR105 so that the top and bottom peak
values are equa! to each other.

7. Tracking offset adjustment

. Set the servo gain adjuster power SW “on” and the rotary selector switch at 2”.

. Place the unit in the Eject mode (tray open).

. Connect a voltmeter to TJ103 on 03 P.C.B (Optical servo).

. Turn power switch ON, and adjust VR106 so that the DC voltage is 0 + 3mV.
