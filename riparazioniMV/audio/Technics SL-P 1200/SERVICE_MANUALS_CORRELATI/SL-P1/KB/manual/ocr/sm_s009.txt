How to remove the laser pickup

. Remove the traverse unit.
. Remove the optical servo P.C.B.

Pull out the connectors (CN101, CN102) of optical
servo P.C.B.

. Remove the bracket setscrew [Fig. 17: @] of pickup

to remove the bracket.
Remove the guide rail retainer setscrew [Fig. 18: @ ]
and pull out the laser pickup from the two guide rails.

*The pickup must be re-adjusted when it is replaced or

fitted after removal.
