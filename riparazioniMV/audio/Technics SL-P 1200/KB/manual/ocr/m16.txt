® Cautions for laser diode light emission

In this unit, the compact disc information is read by the iaser beam diffused from the laser diode in the optical pickup. It is
dangerous to directly look at the laser beam or to let it touch your body. During the normal operation of this unit, the laser diode
will not emit light unless the disc holder is closed and $101 (laser ON/OFF switch) is turned ON. When servicing the unit, note that
the laser diode emits lights if power switch is turned on with S101 ON.
