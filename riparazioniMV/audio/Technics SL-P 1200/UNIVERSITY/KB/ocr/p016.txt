The reset circuit is so designed that it will generate a reset pulse when a disk is inserted.

IC2 detects a rising voltage, and |C4 and !C3 generate a reset pulse.

A reset switch is provided for forced resetting.

1C705, |C6, and |C704 (MN1208) are voltage drop detecting ICs. They output input voltages of up to
about 4.5V, and are grounded if the input voltage drops below about 4.5V, thus protecting microcom-
puter operation. .
