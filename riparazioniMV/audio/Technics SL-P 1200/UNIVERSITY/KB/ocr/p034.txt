8. AN8371S CIRCUIT OPERATION

AN8371S receives an RF signal input from the preceding optical servo |C (AN8370S), and generates

signals (EFM signal and PCK signal) from it to be input to the subsequent digital signal processing LSI
(MN6617).

An internal block diagram is shown in Fig. 8-1. AN8371S consists of an AGC circuit, data slice circuit,
PLL circuit, and dropout detector circuit.
