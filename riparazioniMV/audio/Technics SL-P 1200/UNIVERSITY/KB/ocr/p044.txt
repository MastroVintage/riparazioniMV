Fig. 9-10 AN8371S and LSI Connection Diagram

Fig. 9-10 shows an LS! (MN6617) and PLL IC (AN8371S) connection diagram.
There are four inputs and one output as viewed from the LSI, and the output (CNT2) from the
microcomputer is input to both ICs.
1) PCK, EFM, FPC signals
PCK and EFM signals are as described in Section 6.
FPC signal results from measuring the pulse width of EFM signal (3T to 11T) with PCK signal
(1T).
If EFM signal is a signal of 2T or less, a feedback operation takes place to raise the VCO fre-
quency of the PLL. If EFM signal is 12T or over, the VCO frequency is lowered.
During play (PLL has completed operating), pin 78 of the LSI is constant at 2.5V. If the PLL
is still operating, a control pulse of 2.5V +2.5V is output.
2) SRF, DO signals
SRF signal is generated by data-slicing RF signal (data slice output of AN8371S), and is used
as an input signal for the CLV rough servo (11T servo) in the LSI. The CLV servo is described
later.
