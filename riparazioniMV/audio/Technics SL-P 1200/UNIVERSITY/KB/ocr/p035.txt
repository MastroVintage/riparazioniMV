8-2 PCK Signal

PCK signal is a clock signal synchronized with EFM signal.

EFM signal has some jitter components until it is written into the memory. Therefore, a clock signal
synchronized with EFM signal is necessary in order for the digital signal processing circuit to accurately
receive data.

PCK signal is generated from an EFM signal input by the PLL circuit. Fig. 8-3 shows a timing chart of
EFM signal and PCK signal.

8-3 Other Signals

A part from EFM signal and PCK signal, AN8371S outputs SRF signal and DO signal, and receives input
signals of FPC and SLC.

SRF signal is generated by data-slicing RF signal.

DO signal is a dropout detecting signal for RF signal (high at dropout). Both are input to the CLV control
in LSI (MN6617).

FPC signal is a VCO control signal for the PLL circuit that is fed back from the LSI, and SLC signal is a
slice level control signal from the microcomputer (MN1554CAC).

Their functions are described in 9-3.
