10-3. LPF and De-emphasis Circuit

The lowpass filter (LPF) restores the signals containing harmonics (staircase waves) received from the
deglitch circuit to complete audio signals.

The LPF also has a de-emphasis function.

It reads emphasis or no emphasis from subcode (Q channel), and outputs a control signal from pin 21
(DEMPHA) of 1C601 (LSI). De-emphasis is applied when the output signal is high.
