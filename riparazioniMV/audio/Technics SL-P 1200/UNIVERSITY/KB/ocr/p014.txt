4-1 Required Power for CD Circuit

The following power inputs independent of the power supply for the system are necessary for the CD
circuit.
1) Direct input from battery
2) Input from accessory power circuit
However, illumination power is supplied from the main amplifier for the system.
The power required for the CD circuit is as follows:
1) @ 10V, © 10V
2) © 5V,0 5V
The @) 5V power supply is divided as follows for preventing noise and selection during operation.
1) @ 5V digital power for digital circuit
2) @ 5V analog power for analog circuit IC
3) @ 5V battery power for microcomputer, supplied before disk insertion
4) @ 5V sensor power from accessory power supply to loading sensor

1) ® 10V

2) ® 5V digital, © 5V analog
