2-5 RF Signal Check (RF Signal, Tracking Servo, LS!)

1) When an RF signal is detected, the focus circuit changes from the search mode to the focus servo
mode.

2) In this case, a focus lock signal is applied to the microcomputer, which sends a tracking ON,
command to start the tracking servo circuit.

3) Then, the RF signal is converted to an EFM signal by the PLL IC, and the converted EFM signal
is applied to the LSI. The LSI demodulates the subcode Q data and supplies it to the micro-
computer, which deciphers the TOC and selects the play mode.
