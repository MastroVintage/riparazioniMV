Brake circuit

The brake circuit brakes the tracking actuator to quickly retracts the tracking servo after rapid feed
or kick (10/100).

The process is shown in Fig. 6-12.

The brake circuit applies a single limiter (indicated by the dotted line in the figure) to the TE signal
to absorb the energy after a kick.

The figure shows the case of KICK REV. In the case of KICK FWD, the brake is applied with the
single limiter inverted in polarity. Therefore, the brake circuit requires a kick direction discriminating
signal, which is generated from a tracking cross signal and off track signal as shown in Fig. 6-13.

The cross signal has an advanced phase in KICK FWD, and a delayed phase in KICK REV. Braking
direction is determined by detecting this phase difference.
