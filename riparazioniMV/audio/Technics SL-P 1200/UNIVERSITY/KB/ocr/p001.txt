1. TRAVERSE MECHANISM and OPTICAL PICKUP

1-1 Traverse Mechanism

Fig. 1-1 shows the traverse mechanism.

The traverse mechanism conveys power from the motor to the wormgear, gear A, gear B, and main gear,
and drives the optical pickup with the rack gear.

Gear A and gear B are double clutch gears.

The inner REST switch is closed by the optical pickup base when the optical pickup is returned to the in-

nermost position by the traverse mechanism.
