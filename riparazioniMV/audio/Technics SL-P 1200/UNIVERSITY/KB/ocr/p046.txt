1) BLKCK, CLDCK, SUB Q signals
These three signals transfer the subcode Q data, which has been demodulated from the EFM
data in the LSI, to the microcomputer. The microcomputer deciphers the subcode QO data for
time base control and display.
BLKCK is a clock signal of one block (98 frames) extracted from the EFM data; CLDCK is a
clock signal of one frame similarly extracted, and SUB O becomes subcode OQ data.
Data transfer is serial.
