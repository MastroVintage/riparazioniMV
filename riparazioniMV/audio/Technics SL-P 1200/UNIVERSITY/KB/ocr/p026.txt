In this state, the search signal (triangular
wave) drives the focus coil (raising or
lowering the lens).

When the distance between the disk and
lens is brought close to the focal point by
focus search, the FE signal develops an
S-curve, which is detected to turn search
SW off and FE SW on, thus closing the
focus serve loop.

The auto focus discriminates the state of the servo by RF detection while the focus servo loop is
closed. When RF detection is no longer possible due to defocusing, search SW is turned to start

focus search.

This is input to the microcomputer, and whether the focus is correct can be checked at pin 26

(F LOCK). (Low when the focus is correct.)

Focus error circuit

The diagonal sums (A1 + A2) and (A3+A4) of the 4-divided photo-detector are input to pins 39 and
42. |-V converts the photo-detector output currents to voltages. An external resistor is used for
gain setting. VR101 is a photo-detector balance (PD BAL) adjuster, used to absorb the sensitivity

difference of the photo-detector.

Subtraction of the two converted signals, that is, (A1 + A2) — (A3 + A4), produces a focus error

signal (FE).

This FE signal is routed via the FE amplifier and FE SW to pin 36 (FE OUT).
VR104 is the focus gain (FO GAIN) control, and VR105 the focus offset (FO OFFSET) control.
