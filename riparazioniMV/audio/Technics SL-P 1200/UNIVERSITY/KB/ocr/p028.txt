Tracking error circuit

The tracking error circuit inputs the I-V converted signals to pins 2 and 4 via C119 and C117.

The comparators shape the waveforms.

In the tracking error process of the single beam type, the phase difference between the two signals
is detected and a pulse corresponding to the phase difference is generated as described in 2-3. Deviat-
ing direction is judged from the polarity of the pulse. The generated pulse is smoothed by the LPF
into an analog tracking error (TE) signal. This is how the phase comparator and LPF operate.

Then, the TE signal is output from pin 6 (TE OUT) via the TE amplifier and TE SW.

VR102 is the tracking gain (TR GAIN) control, and VR106 the tracking offset (TR OFFSET)
control.

Kick and hold circuits

Kick means jumping the beam from one track to another during a pause or manual search. Kick
timing is set by a control signal from the microcomputer, and a kick signal is output to pin 20. The
waveform of each kick signal is shown in Fig. 6-10.
