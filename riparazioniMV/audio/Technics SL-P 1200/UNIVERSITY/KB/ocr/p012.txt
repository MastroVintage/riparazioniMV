(1) Sensor input
In disk loading and ejection, the microcomputer controls the loading motor and solenoid with four
sensor inputs.
When a disk is set, the sensor information input to the microcomputer is as follows.

SW3 (P93) 2... . cee ee OV
SW2 (P92) ..... eee OV
Door SW (P83) ..........0.. 5V
Drop detectionSW (P91) ............. 5V

Loading starts as door SW ON information or SW2 ON information switches on the following:
Power supply
Solenoid
Loading motor

Eject starts as the EJECT SW is pressed.

(2) Solenoid control
The solenoid is controlled by PA@ and PA1 outputs.
PA‘ is for starting the solenoid.
PA@ is for holding the solenoid.
The solenoid operates when the disk goes in or out. (OV during operation)

(3) Loading motor control
There are three loading motor control modes.
o Forward operation for loading
o Reverse operation for eject
o Stop
