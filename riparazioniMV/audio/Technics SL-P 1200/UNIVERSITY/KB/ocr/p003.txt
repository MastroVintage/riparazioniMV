If a track pit image is assumed to pass over the 4-divided photo-detector, and deviates to the left or to the
right, a phase difference corresponding to the track deviation is generated between output signals (A1
+ A2) and (A3 + A4). This phase difference is detected by the phase detector, and passed through the
lowpass filter (L.P.F.) to generate a tracking error.
