2-7 Audio Circuit Check (DAC, LPF, Mute)

If no sound is reproduced when the LCD is counting correctly in the play mode, check the audio circuit
and mute circuit from the LSI to DIN pin output.

The 16-bit signals from the LSI are converted to analog signals by the DAC, and branched to right and
left signals by deglitch. The branched signals are routed via the LPFs to be audio signals. Check the route.
(In case of DP3, the signals are then routed via the preamplifier to the main amplifier.)

Then, check the mute circuit that it operates properly.
