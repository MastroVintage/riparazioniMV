7-2 Mode Select Circuit

The multiplexer (1C603) is used for selecting three inputs in mode selection.
1) Servo error voltage selection
The output of pin 1 , 1C601 is applied to the drive circuit when pin 10 and pin 9 of |C 603 are
high.
2) Brake selection by reference voltage
The output of pin 7 , 1C601 is applied to the drive circuit when pin 10 of IC603 is low and
pin 9 of 1C603 is high.
3) Forced forward/reverse selection
The output of pin 8 , 1C601 is applied to the drive circuit when pin 9 of |C603 is high.
The input to pin 9 of 1C603 is controlled by CNT1 to CNT3 of the microcomputer via the NOR circuit.
Thus, pin 9 goes high when all of CNT1 to CNT3 are low (during disk play), or goes low when one of
CNT1 to CNT3 is high (other than during disk play).
The input to pin 10 of |C603 is controlled by CLVS signal 77 of LSI (IC501). Thus, pin 10 goes high
when T11 is OK.
