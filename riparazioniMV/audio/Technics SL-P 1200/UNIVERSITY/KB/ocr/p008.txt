The following are provided as driving devices:
o Loading motor : Turns both ways to load, eject, raise, and lower disks.
o Solenoid : Opens and closes the door, and allows descent on the traverse deck

In the play mode, the traverse deck is set on a free cushion base with the disk held down by the magnet
plate.

* The drop detection switch is a standby switch that is used to recover the magnet plate by means of
EJECT, should it drop when there is no disk loaded.
