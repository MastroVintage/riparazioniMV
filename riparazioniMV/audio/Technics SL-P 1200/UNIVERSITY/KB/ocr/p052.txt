10-1 D—A Converter

1C801 is a D—A converter, which converts
digital signals to analog signals. Digital signals
are input to pins 1 to 16 of 1C801, 16 bits
parallel. Pin 1 (B1) is MSB, and pin 16 (B16)
is LSB. PAM signal is output from pin 17
(OUT).

(Lch = 1 kHz, Rech = 0)

10-2 Deglitch Circuit

The deglitch circuit consists of 1C253 (analog switch), 1C252 (operational amplifier), C255 and C355 (for

hold).

Pin 19 (L DG) of 1C601 (LSI) is the deglitch signal output for the left channel, and pin 20 (R DG) of

1!C601 is the deglitch signal output for the right channel.

Fig. 10-3 shows a demodulation timing chart of the deglitch circuit.

When L DG signal is low, the left channel circuit takes signals in, while the right channel circuit outputs

the signals that have been taken in (and held by C255).
