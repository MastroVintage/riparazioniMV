1-2 3-Beam Type and Single Beam Type

The optical pickup of the SL-XP7 is a single beam type instead of a 3-beam type. The structure of the

optical pickup is shown in Fig. 1-2.

The largest difference in optical structure between the 3-beam type and single beam type is that the single
beam type has no diffraction grating. Therefore, the single beam type has only beam on the optical path,

and its photo-detector is divided into four instead of six (four plus two).

The two sub-beams of the 3-beam type have the function of generating tracking errors. The process of
generating tracking errors by the single beam type that is without sub-beams is described in the following.

Focus errors are generated by the astigma method as in conventional models.
