CNT2 (SLC or TR ON) signal

CNT2 signal is one of the 4-bit control logic signals of the optical servo IC (AN8370S). This

signal remains high until the tracking servo is in the OFF mode (See Table 6-1).

This is used to control both |Cs as follows.

(a) The CLV control in the LSI is kept under speed control even during rapid feed by changing
the rough servo to the traverse tracking servo mode, and when the tracking servo turns on,
the CLV servo is immediately set ready for operation.

(b) The data slice circuit in AN83715S is so controlled that the slice level is forcibly raised to
permit accurate generation of SRF signal as an input signal to the rough servo.
