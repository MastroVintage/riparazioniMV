6-2 Laser APC Circuit

Fig. 6-3 shows the laser APC circuit.

When a laser ON mode command is emitted, pin 5 goes high to turn the laser on.

A loop circuit is formed by O and D (the laser paired with a PIN diode) and the APC in the IC, and laser
beam intensity is detected by the PIN diode in APC operation.

lf laser beam intensity increases, the PIN diode’s photo-electric current increases so that the pin 10
voltage rises (pin 12 voltage is the reference voltage) to decrease laser beam intensity.

6-3. RF Amplifier and Dropout Detector Circuits

(1) RF amplifier circuit
RF signal is the total sum of the 4-divided photo-detector (A1 +A2 +A3 +AA4) of the optical pickup.
These are added in the optical pickup, and are input via Q to pin 33 . (Q is located in the optical
pickup.}
The output is taken out from pin 31, from which it is sent to the next IC for PLL (AN8371S).
The gain between pin 33 andpin 31 is about 34 dB.

(2) Dropout detector circuit
In dropout detection, a dropout on the disk is detected from the above-mentioned RF signal, the
tracking servo is stabilized, and its detection signal is output to pin 32.
The detecting process is shown in Fig. 6-5. The level goes high if a dropout is detected.
