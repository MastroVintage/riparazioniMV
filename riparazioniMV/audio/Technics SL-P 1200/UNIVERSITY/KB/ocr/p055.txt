TROUBLESHOOTING

Note: Do not skip steps in the flow of symptom confirmation.
Keep power on and, if the set is disassembled for troubleshooting, make sure that the symp-
toms are the same as before diassembling the set.
Note: When checking the set with the top cover open, cover the top of the deck to prevent the
optical sensor from wrong operation.
