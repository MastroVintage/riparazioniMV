Control signals and their functions (See Fig. 6-2.)

1) LDON.............., Laser ON command
e Controls laser APC ON/OFF.
2) AFON ............-. Auto focus command

e Controls the focus servo loop to open or close, and the search OSC
switch ON/OFF.

3) TRON .............. Tracking servo command

e Controls the tracking servo loop switch.
4) HOLD .............. Tracking hold command

e Holds tracking error at preceding value during track kick.
5) BRON .............. Tracking brake command

e After high-speed feed or kick, brakes the tracking actuator to absorb
unnecessary vibration in a short time to facilitate tracking.
