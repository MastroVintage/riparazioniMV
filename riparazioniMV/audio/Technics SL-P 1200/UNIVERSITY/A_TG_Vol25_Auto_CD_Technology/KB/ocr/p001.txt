1. Traverse Mechanism And Optical Pick Up

1-1. Traverse Mechanism

The traverse mechanism (Fig. 1-1) drives the optical pickup base by the rack gear which is driven by the
motor via the worm gear, gear A, and gear B. The gear A and gear B are double clutch gears.

The inner REST switch is turned on by the optical pickup base when the optical nickup is driven inner-
most position by the traverse mechanism.
