1) PCK, EFM, FPC signals

—~ PCK and EFM signals have been described in Section C4-3.

FPC signal results from measuring the pulse width of EFM signal (3T to 11T) with PCK signal
(1T).
lf EFM signal is a signal of 2T or less, a feedback operation takes place to raise the VCO fre-
quency of the PLL. If EFM signal is 12T or over, the VCO frequency is lowered.
During play (PLL has completed operating), pin 78 of LSI is constant at 2.5V. If the PLL is being
pulled in, a control pulse of 2.5V +2.5V is output from pin 28.

2) SRF, DO signals
SRF signal is generated by data-slicing RF signal (data slice output of AN8371S), and is input to
the CLV rough servo (11T) servo) of LSI. The CLV servo is described later.
