Tracking error circuit

Tracking error circuit inputs the I-V converted signals to pin 2 and 4 viaC119 and C117. The com-
parators shape the waveforms.

In the tracking error process of the single beam type, the phase difference between the two signals
is detected and a pulse corresponding to the phase difference is generated as described in section. 1-4,
Deviating direction is judged from the polarity of the pulse. The generated pulse is smoothed by the
LPF into an analog tracking error (TE) signal. This is how the phase comparator and LPF operate.
Then, the TE signal is output from pin 6 (TE OUT) via the TE amplifier and TE SW.

VR102 is the tracking gain (TR GAIN) control, and VR106 the tracking offset (TR OFFSET)
control.
