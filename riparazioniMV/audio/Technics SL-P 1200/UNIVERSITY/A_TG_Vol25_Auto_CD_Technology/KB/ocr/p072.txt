C4-3 AN8371S CIRCUIT OPERATION

AN8371S receives the RF signal from the optical servo IC (AN8370S), and generates the EFM signal and
PCK signal for inputting to the digital signal processing LSI (MN6617).

An internal block diagram is shown in Fig. 4-1. AN8371S consists of an AGC circuit, data slice circuit,
PLL circuit, and dropout detector circuit.

C4-3-1 EFM Signal

The RF signal is an analog continuous signal as shown in Fig. 4-2.

Therefore, it cannot be processed as a digital signal unless it is shaped into a digital signal. This waveform
shaping is performed by the data slice circuit, whose output signal is EFM signal.
