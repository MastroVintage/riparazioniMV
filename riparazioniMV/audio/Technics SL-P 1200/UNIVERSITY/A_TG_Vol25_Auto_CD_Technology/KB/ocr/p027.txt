4. CIRCUIT OPERATIONS (TYPE A)
4-1 Traverse Control Circuit (Fig. 4-1)

To the optical pickup at the starting position, RF must be present to operate the traverse motor. The IN
SW changes (H) > (L) > (H) by the movement of optical pickup block.

When SW4 becomes (L), and IN SW is (H). TRV R (L) and TRV F (H) are output from 1C601, the
traverse motor drives the optical pick from outside to inside.

When IN SW turns (L), TRV R turns (H) and TRV F turns (L), and the optical pick moves from inside to
outside. When IN SW is (H) again, the optical pick up is at the starting position, the traverse motor has
ended the starting position setting operation, i.e. both TRV R and TRV F have become (L).

During the setting operation CNT 1, 2, 3, and 4 are all at (H); but just as soon as completion of the opera-
tion, CNT 1 remains at (H), while CNT 2, 3, and 4 change to (L).
