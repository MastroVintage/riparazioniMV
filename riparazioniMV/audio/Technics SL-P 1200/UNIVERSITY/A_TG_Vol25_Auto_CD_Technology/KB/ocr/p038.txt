A4-4 Description of New Servo Circuit
(MN8373S and MN8374S)

1. General

The servo circuit in CQ-E850, CQ-DP25/26
has been entirely

changed with new IC's for
performance improvement and
compactness.

2. Circuit Configuration

The servo circuit used in the
conventional FF-1 CD players comprised a
servo IC (AN8370S) and discrete circuit.
In contrast, the modified servo circuit
consists of three IC chips with on
discrete circuit.

(1). IC-1 (servo amplifier)
=Processes analog servo signals.
(2). IC-601 (servo controller)
=Processes digital servo signals.
(3). IC-602 (servo driver)
=Drives the focus, tracking, and
traverse coils.
The block diagram of IC-1 (AN8373S) and

IC-601 (AN8374S) are shows figure 4-9.

operations are almost identical to those
