A4-3. Spindle Motor Circuit
When disk detection ended, the spindle motor starts to rotate. The focus servo and the traverse servo are
commanding the spindle motor operation; and after reading the TOC, play starts.

If there is any trouble in focus or traverse servo, it will be become EJECT mode.

When the SENSE pulse appears, CNT 1, 2, 3, and 4 are (H). MLD, MCLK, and the MDATA are output
from the micro-computer. The spindle motor starts to run by outputting the CLV data at pin 64 PC (L)
of 10501 to IC2.

When the number of revolution increased, LD ON and AF ON signals (CNT 1, (L), 2, 3, and 4 (H)) are
output from the micro-computer.

When the micro-computer receives the FLOCK (L) signal from 1C601, the TRON signal (CNT 1, 2 (L), 3,
and 4 (H)) is output from the micro-computer.

The TRON signal forms tracking servo while applying brake, then to the CLV control along with the
rough servo by SRF signal of |C601 which is obtained from the RF signal.

When this circuit changes from the rough servo to the refined servo. CLVS of I1C501 turns (H); and the
PLAY signal (CNT 1, 2, 3, and 4 (H)) is output from CPU. At this time, reading of the TOC starts.
1. At the head of lead-in track, it positions by kick pusle while reading the data.
2. It reads TOC in the micro-computer.
(If there is any abnormality, it will reject at the place where abnormal function is affirmed.)
3. Play starts from the track 1.
