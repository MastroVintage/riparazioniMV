C4-3-2  PCK Signal

The PCK signal is a clock signal synchronized with EFM signal.

The EFM signal includes a jitter component until it is written into the memory. Therefore, a clock signal
synchronized with EFM signal is necessary in order for the digital signal processing circuit to accurately
receive data.

The PCK signal is generated from the PLL circuit with an EFM signal input. Fig. 4-3 shows a timing chart
of EFM signal and PCK signal.

C4-3-3 Other Signals

IC AN8371S outputs SRF signal and DO signal besides the EFM and PCK signals. FPC and SLC signals
are input signals to 1C AN83715S.

The SRF signal is generated by data-slicing RF signal. The DO signal is the dropout detecting signal in
the RF signal (high at dropout). Both are input to the CLV control LS! (MN6617).

The FPC signal is the VCO control signal of the PLL circuit that is fed back from the LSI. The SLC signal
is the slice level control signal from the microcomputer (MN1554CAC).

Their functions are described in Section 9-3.
