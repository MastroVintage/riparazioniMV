12 samples of data (six each for the right and left) are taken as a frame for signal processing. In
other words, 24-symbol data constitutes a frame. W1 to W24 shown in Fig. 4-3 correspond to
it.

Data W1 to W24 are first scrambled by C2. Scramble may be considered to be a small interjeave.
In C2 scramble, the data are delayed over two frames alternately in terms of sample data.

After C2 scramble, the data enter the C2 encoder, which generates 4-symbol parity signals
Q1 to Q4 from the 24-symbol data using reed solomon code, and arranges them in the frame
center.

The symbol data count in one frame is 28 in total.

Next is the interleave.

As shown in the figure, the interleave delays the 28-symbol data, including the Q parity signals,
over the range of 4 frames minimum to 108 frames maximum.

The C1 encoder generates 4-symbol parity signals P1 to P4 from the 28-symbol data, which
have been rearranged by interleaving, using reed solomon code, and arranges them at the frame
end.

Here, the symbol data count in a frame is 32 in total. (See Fig. 4-3.)
