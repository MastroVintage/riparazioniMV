C4-2-2. Mode Select Circuit

The multiplexer (I[C603) is used for mode selection.
1) Servo error voltage selection (Play mode)
The output from pin 1, 1C601 is applied to the drive circuit when pin 10 and pin 9 of IC603 are
high.
2) Brake selection by reference voltage (KICK mode)
The output from pin 7, 1C601 is applied to the drive circuit when pin 10 of IC603 is low and
pin 9 of 1C603 is high.
3) Forced forward/reverse selection (SKIP mode)
The output from pin 8, 1C601 is applied to the drive circuit when pin 9 of C603 is high.
The input to pin 9 of 1C603 is controlled by CNT 1 to CNT3 of the microcomputer via the NOR circuit.
Thus, pin 9 goes high when all of CNT1 to CNT3 are low (during disk play), or goes low when one of
CNT1 to CNTS3 is high (other than during disk play).
The input to pin 10 of IC603 is controlled by CLVS signal from pin 77 of LSI (1C501). Thus, pin 10 goes
high when T11 is OK.

C4-2-3 Traverse Drive Circuit

The traverse drive circuit consists |C602, Q606 to Q609 to drive the traverse motor using the reference
voltage applied to pin 3 of IC602 and the polarity corresponding to the voltage difference applied to
pin 2. Q606 to Q609 comprise a dual direction drive amp. Q607 and Q609 are NPN and O607 and O608
are PNP. By controlling the polarity of the voltage applied to the Bases of ALL TRANSISTORS, it will

determine whether the motor is supplied with a (—) or (+) voltage thus determining the direction of motor
movement.
