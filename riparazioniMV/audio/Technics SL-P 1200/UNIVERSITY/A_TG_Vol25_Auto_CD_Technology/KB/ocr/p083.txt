1) BLKCK, CLDCK, SUB Q signal
These three signals transfer the subcode O data demodulated from the EFM data by LSI! to the
microcomputer. The microcomputer decodes the subcode Q data and carries out time base
control and display.
The BLKCK is a clock signal of one block (98 frames) extracted from EFM data; the CLDCK
is a clock signal of one frame similarly extracted, and the SUB Q becomes subcode O data.
Data are transferred serially.
