When traverse motor malfunctions. Check IN SW (H).

Also, when the IN SW is not in changing stage, check the system CPU Pins 18 and 19 (TRV R, TRV
F), and Pins 14-17 (CNT1, 2, 3, 4, all H) outputs to 1C601, then from 1C601 (TRV F/R) to IC602,
from |C602 to the traverse motor.

When the focus lens moves up and down repeatedly, check if the disk is properly set. If the LD ON
terminal of Pin 28 IC601 is (H), check the LD terminal of Pin 7 IC1. To see if APC circuit is turned
on to intensify the laser.

Check connections between the photo-detector and IC1, check if there is a pulse at the FE terminal
of Pin 15 1C1, and a pulse at SENSE terminal of Pin 24 1C601.

If the disk ejects, when CNT1 is (H), CNT2, 3, and 4 are (L). Check to see if the CLW terminal of Pin
10 1C601, the FPO terminal of Pin 24 1C1, and FD terminal of Pin 10 1C602 generate the triangular
wave respectively.

If the disk ejects at the time when the disk detection ended (CNT1, 2, 3, and 4 (H)), check connec-
tion between IC2 and spindle motor, |C2 and 1C501 Pin 32 L/R (Clock pulse), Pin 65 EC (Start-up
control pulse) and Pin 64 PC (L)).

Also trace and check Pin 23 MLD, Pin 24 MCLK and Pin 25 MDATA (data pulse) of the micro-
computer.

If the disk ejects at the time when CNT1 is (L), CNT2, 3, and 4 are (H), check to see if 1C601 Pin 26
FLOCK IS (H > L). Check connection between IC601 and IC1 Pin 32 RFDET (L), Pin 31 BDD and
between IC1 Pin 1 AMP1 and optical pickup.

If the disk ejects when CNT1, 2 are (L), CNT3, and 4 are (H)},

1) check the tracking servo circuit, and check connection between tracking coil and Pin 41 IC601,
and Pin 27 VREF (2.5V) of 1C601, connection between IC1 and 1C601 [IVA (Pin 13) > LSA
(Pin 1)] [IVB (14) > LSB (2)] [TPI (17) — TEO (6)] [TGC (25) (OV) « VDET (23)] [TPO
(22) > BRIN (42)].

2) check the operation from the RF output to the CLV Control.
Also check if there is RF signal at AFR of Pin 35 IC1, at SRF of Pin 19 1C601 and also check
TRON (L) Pin 10, STAT (L) Pin 11 and CLVS Pin 77 (L > H) of IC501.

After CNT1, 2, 3, and 4 became (L), it will not read TOC and the disk ejects, eheck if there is SUBO
pulse output from Pin 3 1C501, EFM pulse from Pin 21 1C601. Check KICK at Pin 27 1C601 and
TVPO output at Pin 39 IC601. If SUBQ is output the AMS (auto-music scan) is operated by the
Kick Pulses then read TOC in the micro-computer.
