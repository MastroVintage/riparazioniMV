The MASH block operates at 32fs, where
fs is the sampled input _— signal
frequency. This means that a 32 fold
oversampling filter is required. On the
MN6623, however, the first digital
filter oversamples the fs signal into a
4fs signal. The following MASH block
then performs zero-order holding on the
Afs signal to convert it into a 32fs
signal.

The digital filter and MASH blocks use
384fs and 64fs clocks, respectively, to
perform time-division multiplexing on
the left and right channel signals. The
PWM block uses a 768fs clock to process
the left and right signals
independently. The MASH block converts
the 16 bits, 32fs signal into 11 values,
which after Pulse-Width Modulation in
the PWM block become analog signals. The
following section describe the function
of each MN6623 block.
