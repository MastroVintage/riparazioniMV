Brake circuit

The brake circuit brakes the tracking actuator to quickly retract the tracking servo after rapid feed
or kick (10/100). The process is shown in Fig. 4-12.

The brake circuit applies a single limiter (indicated by the dotted line in the figure) to the TE signal
to absorb the energy after kick.

The figure shows the brake in the KICK REV mode. In the KICK FWD mode, the brake is applied
with the single limiter inverted in polarity. Therefore, the brake circuit requires a kick direction
discriminating signal, which is generated from a tracking cross signal and off track signal as shown in
Fig. 4-13.

The cross signal phase leads in the KICK FWD mode and lags in the KICK REV mode. The brake
direction is determined by detecting the above phase difference.
