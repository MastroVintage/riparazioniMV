When a laser ON mode command (H, L, L, L) is received, pin 5 goes high to turn the laser on.

A loop circuit is formed by Q and D (the laser paired with a PIN diode) and the APC in the IC, and laser

rises (Pin 12 voltage is the reference voltage) to decrease laser beam intensity.

C4-1-3. RF Amplifier and Dropout Detector Circuits

(1) RF amplifier circuit
RF signal is the total sum of all the 6-segment photo-detector (A1 + A2 + A3 + A4 + B1 + B2) of
the optical pickup. The resulting RF signal is applied to pin 33, via Q, (OQ is located in the optical
pickup.) where it is amplified by an RF amplifier having gain of approx 34 dB.
The output of the RF amplifier appears at pin 31, and couples to pin 15 of |1C AN8371S.

(2) Dropout detector circuit
The dropout detector detects signal dropouts in the RF signal, stabilizes the tracking servo against
dropouts, and provides the defection signal to pin 32.
The detection dropout process is shown in Fig. 6-5. The level goes high if a dropout is detected.
