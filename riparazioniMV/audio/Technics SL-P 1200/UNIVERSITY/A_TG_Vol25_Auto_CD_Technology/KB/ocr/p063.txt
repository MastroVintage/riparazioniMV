Focus error circuit

The diagonal sums (A1 + A2) and (A3 + A4) of the 4-segment photo-detector are input to pins 39
and 42 of 1C101. The I/V convertor converts the photo-detector output currents to voltages. An ex-
ternal resistor is used for gain setting. VR101 is a photo-detector balance (best eye) adjuster, for
setting off the sensitivity difference of the photo-detector.

The difference between the two converted signals (A1 + A2) — (A3 + A4) is a focus error signal (FE).
The FE signal applied to pin 36 (FE out) of 1C101 via the FE amplifier and FE SW.

VR104 is the focus gain (FO GAIN) control, and VR105 the focus offset (FO OFFSET) control.
