10. Traverse Servo Circuit Operation

PLAY mode:

The tracking error signal applied
to IC-601 pin 42 goes through the
traverse switch and TRV (traverse
circuit) filter (for response
compensation) and appears at pin
39. This output is used to
control the traverse actuator.

KICK mode:

The KICK signal decoded from the
system CPU command by the control
logic appears at pin 27. It is
used to control the tracking
actuator which KICKs the pick-up
by a span of 1, 10, or 100 tracks
according to the system CPU
command.

Forced traverse mode:

The TRVF, TRVR signals supplied
from system CPU to pins 34 and
35, respectively, are decoded to
produce a traverse drive signal.
The signal appears at pin 25 and
drives the traverse coil.

11. RF Signal Detection and Auto Focus
Circuit

The auto focus circuit within IC-601
performs focus search. If it detects an
FE signal S-Curve it closes the focus
switch contacts to close the focus servo
loop. At this point the RF detector
within IC-101 detects the RF signal and
supplies it to the F.LOCK DET. input at
IC-601 pin 36.

The F.LOCK signal is used to indicate to
system CPU that focus is "IN". If focus
goes "OUT" and the RF signal is no longer
detected, the auto focus circuit opens
the focus loop switch and starts focus
search again.

12. Dropout Detection Circuit
(1). Black Band Dropout (BDO)

Dropouts are detected from the RF
Signal within Ic-101. In order to
stabilize the tracking servo against
dropout; the BDO signal is supplied
from IC-601 pin 37 to the phase
difference limitter and used to hold
the tracking servo operation.

- Signal Dropout (SDO)
If an RF signal dropout is detected
by the RF detector, or a BDO is
detected, the SDO signal is supplied
to the signal processor to stabilize
the CLV servo operation.

The SDO signal is supplied to the
signal processor to stabilize the
CLV servo operation. The SDO signal,
produced within IC-601, is used to
stabilize PLL circuit operation.
