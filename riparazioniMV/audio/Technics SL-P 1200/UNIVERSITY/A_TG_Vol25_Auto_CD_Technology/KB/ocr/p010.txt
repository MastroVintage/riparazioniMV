Feeder Arm Ass’y

When the disc moves beyond SW2 (SW2: ON), the solenoid is turned off, and the hook is
released.

The weight of the disc lowers the feeder arm assembly slightly. Its lowering force rotates the
cam gear slightly counterclockwise, then the cam gear and loading gear are engaged.

The loading gear rotates clockwise and the cam gear rotates counterclockwise.

The rotation of the cam gear lowers the feeder arm assembly and turns on SW4.

The disc also lowers and is set on the turntable of optical pickup.
