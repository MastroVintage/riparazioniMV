If a track pit image runs over the 4-segment photo-detector and deviates to the left or the right, a phase
difference corresponding to the track deviation is produced between the output signals (A1 + A2) and (A3
+ A4). This phase difference is detected by the phase detector, and passed through the lowpass filter
(L.P.F.) to generate a tracking error signal.
