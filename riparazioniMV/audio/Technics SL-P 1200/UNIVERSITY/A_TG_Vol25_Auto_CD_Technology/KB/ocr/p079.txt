Fig. 4-8 Playback Signal Processing

The flow of playback signal processing is just opposite to the flow of recording system signal process-
ing. Here, decoders for error detection and correction are used in place of the C1 and C2 encoders
that are used for parity generation.

Both the C1 and C2 decoders are capable of error detection and correction. If data that cannot be
corrected by C1 decoder is marked with C1 flag, and after deinterleaving, the data is sent to the C2
decoder for accurate correction.

If data that cannot be corrected by C2 decoder, the average value obtained from the adjacent data is
switched to interpolation after the process of C2 unscramble.
