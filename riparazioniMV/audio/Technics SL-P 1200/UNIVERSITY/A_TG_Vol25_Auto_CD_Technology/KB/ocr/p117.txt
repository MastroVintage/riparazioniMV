A6-3. CLEANING PROCEDURE FOR THE FOCUS LENS

Symptoms
The following symptoms may be caused not only by faulty software or hardware but also by the
soiled focus lens.
@ Not reading TOC
@ Not playback
@ Skipping sound

Cause
Soiling of the focus lens surfaces with dirt, dust, finger print, and others.

(Note) Clean the lens in the following steps even if it looks to be clean because soiling is not always
discernible visually.
