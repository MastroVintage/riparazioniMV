The digital filter block consists of
three sub-blocks: ATT, FIR1, and FIR2
as shows in figure 5-10. It performs
digital attenuation and oversampling to
convert the input 16 bits, fs data into
a 16 bits, 4fs signal.

The ATT attenuates the input data ina
1/64 increment by multipling the data by
any 6 bits data. FIR1 and FIR2 are
2-fold oversampling digital filters with
85 and 21 taps each. Four-fold
oversampling is accomplished by

cascading the two -fold oversampling
filters.
