(2) Unloading the disk from the magazine
When the loading motor rotates to the loading direction, the solenoid turns on, the gear lock lever

goes down and engages with the cam gear. Then the cam gear pushes the disk ejecting lever once. As
a result, the magazine lever is pushed, and kicks the disk out to the loading mechanism.
