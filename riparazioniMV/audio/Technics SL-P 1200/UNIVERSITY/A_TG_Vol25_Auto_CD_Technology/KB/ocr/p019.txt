CD CHANGER LOADING MECHANISM (TYPE B)

B3-1 Elevator Motor and Disk Loading Positioning

When the magazine placed on the elevator base with cover closed, the elevator motor (M1) runs, and the
elevator base moves downward until it hits SW2 and stop.
The elevating process is disk positioning.

1 The stop position at the SW2 is the initial position of Disc Loading.
2 For moving the magazine to the correct loading position, it is detected by the pulse of 70 interval.
