C4-1 AN8370S CIRCUIT OPERATION

The optical servo reads the signals recordec on a disk using the laser beam.

AN8370S is the main IC of the optical servo system consisting of laser APC, RF signal amplifier, focus servo
(including auto focus), tracking servo (including kick), drop output detector, and the control logic that
controls the optical servo in this IC.
