Sensor input

During disk loading or ejection operation, the microcomputer controls the loading motor and sole-
noid with four sensor inputs.

When a disk is set, the sensor information input to the microcomputer is as follows.

SW3 (P93) ..........08, OV
SW2 (P92) ............, OV
Door SW (P83) ..........00. 5V
Drop detection SW (P91) ............. 5V

Loading starts when door SW or SW2 switches on the following:
Power supply
Solenoid
Loading motor

Eject starts when EJECT SW is pressed.

Solenoid control
The solenoid is controlled by PA@ and PA1 outputs.

PA1 is for starting the solenoid.

PA@ is for holding the solenoid. —~
The solenoid operates when the disk goes in or out. (OV during operation)

Loading motor control

There are three loading motor control modes.
o Forward operation for loading
o Reverse operation for eject

o Stop
