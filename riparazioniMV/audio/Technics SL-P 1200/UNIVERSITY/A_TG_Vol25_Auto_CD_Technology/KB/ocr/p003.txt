1-3. Photo Detector

The photo Detector is probably the single most important component in the campact disc player. The
reason for this is that all intelligence needed to operate the unit is developed from the returning laser beam
by the photo detector. This will include focus error signal, tracking error signal as well as the intelligence
signal. °

The single beam type (Fig. 1-3b) has a large PD surface area to prevent RF signal level reduction. However,
if the beam irradiated at a scratch or other defects on the disc surface, the servo errors occur and decrease
playability.

In order to compensate such divergent conditions, the photo-detector structure shown in Figure 1-3c is

used, the four parts in the center are used for error detection, and the total sum including the periphery
(B1 + B2) is the RF signal.
