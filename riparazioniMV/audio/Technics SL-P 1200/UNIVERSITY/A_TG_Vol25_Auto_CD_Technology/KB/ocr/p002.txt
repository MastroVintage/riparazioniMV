The largest difference in optical structure between the 3-beam type and single beam type is that
single beam type has no diffraction grating. Therefore, the single beam type has only one beam on
optical path, and its photo-detector is divided into four instead of six {four plus two).

The two sub-beams of the 3-beam type have the function of generating tracking errors signal. The
process of generating tracking error signal by the single beam type that is without sub-beams is
described in Section 1-4. Focus error signal generated by the conventional astigma method.

3-Beam type has a small RF detector area (150 um squared). Therefore, it has a disadvantage when
playing discs with fingerprints.

1-Beam type (4-segments) overcomes the fingerprint playability but it has a disadvantage when
playing scratched discs. The area of the 4-segment photodetector is large (1000 um squared).
Therefore, the error generated by fingerprints is included and corrected. Since the focus and track-
ing detectors are included as part of the RF detector, the spike is detected as an error signal.

1-Beam type (6-segments) has a reduced size RF detector (250 um squared) and two large and
separate detectors have been added. Any spikes generated by a scratched disc do not appear as a
large spikes to the tracking and focus detectors. The total area of the photodector is now 1400 um
squared.
