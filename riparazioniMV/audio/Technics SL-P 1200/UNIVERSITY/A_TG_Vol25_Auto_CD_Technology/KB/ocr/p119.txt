B6 TROUBLESHOOTING (TYPE B)
B6-1 CAUTION
1. When making any connections, be sure to turn off all power source.

2. This changer is exclusive for 12 cm CD. Therefore, it cannot play 8 cm CD even with some form of
adaptor.

3. For enabling the disk moves smoothly in the magazine, check that the CD is free of burr.

Display surface Burr when disk is taken out from the die.

Ng Touch the corner and if the disk has burr,

- remove it with emery paper, etc.

4. When operating the changer main body, be sure to close the cover. If the disk detecting sensor is
exposed to the direct sun-light the sensor will malfunction.

5. Sound skipping due to vibration
Since the CD autochanger, is mounted in the trunk, countermeasure for vibration is considered.
However, when the car is driving on a very rough road conditions will sometimes cause the player to
skip the music. However, the disc and the CD Player will not be damaged by these conditions.
(Example: Pothole, railroad crossing, road paved with stone, etc.)
