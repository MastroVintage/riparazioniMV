When the optical pickup starting position is set, this circuit makes focus search and detects the disk.
The function is finished by the SENSE pulse (a disk exists) from 1C601 pin 24.

By the riser edge from (L) to (H) of IN SW, disk detecting instruction (CNT 1 (H)}, 2, 3 and 4 (L)) is
output from the micro-computer. Following this instruction |C601 instructs search oscillation and laser
emission.

The search oscillation (triangular wave) appears at pin 10, along with FE out at pin 9, feed to IC1 pin 16.
The output at pin 23, from the FPO terminal passes through the filter of 1C1, drives the focus coil in
IC602. As a result, the optical pickup moves up and down.

Meantime, the laser emission instruction LD ON at pin 28 of 1C601 joins the LD APC circuit at pin 31 of
1C1 LD ON. The laser power supplies at pin 7 of |C1, the laser emits.

The focus control circuit detects this state with the photo-detector and joins the FE signal at pin 15 of
iC1 into 1C601. And it detects the “S-curve” when the proper disk exists, and search oscillation as well as

laser emission are normal. The detecting operation ends when (L) > (H) pulse from the SENSE applied
to the micro-computer.
