5. Tracking offset adjustment

A. Purpose

As with the focus offset adjustment, the tracking offset adjustment is necessary to set the reference point
of the tracking servo operating range.

The only difference is that the tracking servo compensate for horizontal control as opposed to vertical
control by the focus servo,

B. Test disc, RF waveform and TE (Tracking Error) signal

The test disc, RF waveform, and TE signal waveform are the same as described in the focus offset
adjustment. The only difference is the TE signal test point.
