I. MECHANICAL ADJUSTMENTS

™ When the optical pick-up and peripheral mechanism parts incorporated in the traverse deck are replaced
or removed, such as the spindle motor, it is necessary to confirm the adjustment related to the optical
pick-up and then readjust as required.

1. Turntable Height Adjustment
A. Purpose

The outjut from the laser diode is focused on the signal side of the disc, via the focus lens. Focussing is
accomplished by moving the focus lens vertically. The focus servo is used to control such vertical movement.
Since the focus servo is designed to operate within a limited range, it is necessary to adjust the lens at the
mechanical midpoint position of the servo’s respective operating range. If this adjustment should be
deviated, skipping may occur during play or in an extreme case, no turntable rotation.
