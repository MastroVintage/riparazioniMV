C. Basic principle of adjustment

Due to distance variations between the disc surface and the lens caused by uneven discs, the laser beam
covering a given pit varies in diameter. When the lens is close to the Disc the diameter is large and covers
adjacent pits. The signal from adjacent pits causes interference and the RF becomes distorted.
