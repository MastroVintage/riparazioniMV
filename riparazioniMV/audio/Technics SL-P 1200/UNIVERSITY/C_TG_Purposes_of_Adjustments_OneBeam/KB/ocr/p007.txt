2. Focus gain adjustment

A. Purpose

Regardless of disc warpage (+/— 0.4mm) due to manufacturing tolerances, the focus servo must
operate to constantly keep the lens focused on the disc’s pits. The greater the servo’s gain, the
easier the lens can focus on the disc; however it becomes unstable due to unwanted signals. The

gain is therefore set to unity (0 dB=1, @750Hz) at which point stability is acceptable and the gain
is adequate.
