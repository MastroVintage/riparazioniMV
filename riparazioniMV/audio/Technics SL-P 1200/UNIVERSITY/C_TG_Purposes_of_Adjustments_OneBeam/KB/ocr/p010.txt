4. Focus offset adjustment

A. Purpose

The CD player must be able to compensate for manufacturing imperfections of disc and dust or any other
contaminants on the signal side.

As the pick-up traces the track, if dust or disc imperfections are present, the focusing depth changes.
Without proper focus the tracking servo is affected as well;causing the pick-up to move to a different track.
When the lens detects imperfections as mentions above, the focus servo is enabled in order to maintain
proper focusing within the operating range of the servo.

The focus offset alignment is therefore necessary to adjust the servo’s operating reference point.
