6. Tracking offset balance adjustment
A. Purpose
During playback operation, the laser beam must follow the track exactly at its center.

If it is not, proper tracking can not be accomplished, and skipping may occur during playback of the
disc which has manufacturing imperfections and dust or any other contaminants on the signal side.

B. Basic principle of adjustment

A 1 KHz signal is applied to the tracking servo circuit to intentionally cause the beam to oscillate from
side to side of the track being traced (see fig. 2-6-1). This is done to simulate an error signal so that the
tracking servo can be adjusted for minimum gain (minimum swing) at 1 KHz.
