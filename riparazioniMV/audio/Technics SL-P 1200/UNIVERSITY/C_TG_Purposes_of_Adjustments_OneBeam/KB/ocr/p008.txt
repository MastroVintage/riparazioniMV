B. Principle of the focus gain adjustment

The focus servo loop is opened, at which point a 750 Hz signal is applied to simulate the acceptable
condition. This point is TP2 on the “servo gain adjuster’.

The signal, once through the focus servo, appears at TP1. The focus gain variable resistor is used to set
the voltage at TP1 to equal the level at TP2; at which point the gain is one (0 dB).

The reason why the focus servo must be adjusted using a 750 Hz tone is because this is the frequency
that causes the gain to become unity (OdB=1).
