3. Tracking gain adjustment

A. Purpose

During disc manufacturing, disc eccentricity results due to tooling tolerances. Eccentricity also results if
the disc does not rest exactly in the center of the CD player’s spindle.
The tracking servo is designed to overcome these tolerances and keep the lens on track.

B. Principle of the Tracking gain adjustment

The tracking servo loop is opened at which point a 1KHz, 1.2KHz or 1.5KHz signal is fed. This point is
TP2 of the “servo gain adjuster’.

The signal, once through the servo, appears at TP1. The tracking gain control is to be adjusted so that the
level of TP1 equals that of TP2, at which point the gain is one (OdB)

The reason why the focus servo to be adjusted using a 1KHz, 1.2KHz or 1.5KHz tone is because these are
the frequencies that cause the gain to become unity (OdB=1).
