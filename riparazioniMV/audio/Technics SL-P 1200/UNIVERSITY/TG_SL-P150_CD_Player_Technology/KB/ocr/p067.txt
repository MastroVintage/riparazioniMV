(b) PCK Signal

The PCK signal is a clock signal in
synchronous with the EFM signal. The EFM
signal includes a jitter component to
some degree. Therefore the clock signal
in synchronous with the EFM signal is
required for transmitting the EFM signal
exactly to the digital signal processing
circuit.

The PLL circuit receives the EFM signal
and forms the PCK signal. Figure 17-29
shows the timing chart of the EFM signal
and the PCK signal.
