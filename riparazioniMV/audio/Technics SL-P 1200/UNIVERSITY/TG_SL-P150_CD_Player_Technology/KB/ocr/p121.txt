22. Reference

1. Mechanism Adjustment

When the optical pickup and peripheral
mechanism parts incorporated in the
traverse deck are replaced or removed,
such as the spindle motor, it is
necessary to confirm the adjustment
related to the optical pickup and then
readjust as required.

A.Turntable Height Adjustment

a) Purpose
The output from the laser diode is
focused on the signal side of the
disc, via the focus lens. Focussing is

accomplished by moving the focus lens
vertically. The focus servo is used to
control such vertical movement.

Since the focus servo is designed to
Operate within a limited range, it is
necessary to adjust the lens at the
mechanical midpoint position of the
servo's respective operating range. If
this adjustment should be deviated,
skipping may occur during play mode or
in an extreme case, no turntable
rotation, etc.
