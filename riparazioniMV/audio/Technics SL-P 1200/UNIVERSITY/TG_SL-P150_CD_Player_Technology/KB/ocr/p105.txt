2-4. Disc Loader Elevation Mechanism

The function of the elevation mechanism
is to drive the disc loader to the
selected disc tray.

After a disc has been specified, the
ELEVATOR MOTOR drives the ELEVATOR GEAR
so that the LOADING UNIT is set at the
height of the specified disc. shows
figure 21-7.

The bottom of the ELEVATOR GEAR has a
SHUTTER with two openings which operate
the optical sensing circuit shows figure
21-8.

Two pulses are generated for each
rotation of the ELEVATOR GEAR. The DISC
LOADER is elevated from one disc to the
next each time the ELEVATOR GEAR makes a
single revolution.

The purpose of this sensing system is so
that the system CPU can determine the
location of the ELEVATOR UNIT.

A REST POSITION switch (S454) is provided
to sense the lowest position of the
ELEVATOR UNIT. This position corresponds
to the position at which the disc rests
on the turntable.
