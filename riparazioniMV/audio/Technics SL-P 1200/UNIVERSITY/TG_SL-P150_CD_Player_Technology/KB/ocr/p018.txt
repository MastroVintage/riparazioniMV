In the example it is assumed that the

If three zeros 000 are selected as
junction bits (see figure 12-2 a),
counting starts with 5 positive channel
bits, at transient 4 negative channel
bits. The DSV (t1) of the data block is
DSV (tl) = (-3) + (45) + (-4) + (46) +
(-2) = +2

In this case there is a positive DC
level in the output signal.

By adding the junction bits 001, and

starting with the same DSV (to)= -3, the
result become (-3) + (+2) + (-3) + (44)
+ (-6) + (+2) = -4 (see figure 12-2 b).

Thus, the result becomes negative, so
the DC content in the output signal will
be negative too : DSV (tl) = -4.
