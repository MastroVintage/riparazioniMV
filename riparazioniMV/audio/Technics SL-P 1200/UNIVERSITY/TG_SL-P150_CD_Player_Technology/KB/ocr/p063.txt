What is a Linear Velocity?

The compact disc uses a constant linear
veloocity (CLV) drive system. This means
that the disc speed changes to maintain
a constant reading speed approx 1.3 m/s.
In figure 17-1. shows the distance from
A to B is shorter than from C to D.

To read this data at a constant speed
the disc must rotate slower at the outer
periphery. The range is 500 rpm through

200 rpm from inner to outer periphery.
The proper speed is maintained by
reading the synchronizing signal
(7.35KHz) and comparing it to a
reference signal.

As pointed out before, the data on the
disc is recorded in blocks, which we
called "frame". Each frame starts with a
unique sync word, which is used to
cntrol the spindle motor.

The frequency of these sync words is
compared against a fixed frequency

(derived from a crystal oscillator) ina
phase comparator and the motor is driven
according to any frequency or phase
difference.
