15) Description of Modified Servo Circuit
(MN8373S and MN8374S)

1. General

The servo circuit in CD player with the
standard mechanism (SL-P150, SL-P200,
SL-P250, etc) has been entirely modified
during production. The units containing
modified servo circuit can be identified
as follows:

(1). Serial number suffix: Refer to
supplement.
(2). A caution label sticking: THE

ORIGINAL DESIGN HAS BEEN CHANGED.

2. Circuit Configuration

The Servo circuit used in the
conventional FF-1 CD players comprised a
servo IC (AN8370S) and discrete circuit.
In contrast, the modified servo circuit
consists of three IC chips with on
discrete circuit.

(1). IC-101 (servo amprifier)
=Processes analog servo signals.
(2). IC-102 (servo controller)
=Processes digital servo signals.
(3). IC-103 (servo driver)

=Drives the focus,
traverse coils.
