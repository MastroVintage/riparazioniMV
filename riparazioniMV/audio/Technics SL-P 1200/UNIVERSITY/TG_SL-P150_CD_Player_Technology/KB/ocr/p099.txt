analog signals with 11 different pulse
widths which are precision controlled by
a crystal controlled oscillator.

The pulse width modulation (PWM) samples
the input signal along the time axis and
yields pulses with pulse widths that
correspond to sampled signal amplitudes.
The resulting pulse signal contains
frequency components similar to those
obtained with the PAM technique.
