7-2 Audio Parameters

The information area on the CD is
divided in three parts:
-The lead-in track;

-The audio program area;
-The lead-out track;

The audio program area consists of 16-
bits wide words, encoded as 2's
complement numbers. The lead-in and
lead-out tracks consist of digital
zero's.

The audio information can be recorded
with a linear frequency characteristic
(no emphasis), ora first-order pre-
emphasis characteristic. In the latter
case, the time constants are 50 microsec
and 15 microsec.

The player detects via 4 control bits in
the data stream whether or not emphasis
is applied and switch the de-emphasis
circuit accordingly.
