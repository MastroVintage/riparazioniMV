| 3-2. Disc Loading Operation

After the magazine has been loaded, the

elevator unit remains in the "standby"
mode, until a disc is selected. The
following example will describe the

operation sequence to select the third
disc and play it shows figure 21-12.

(1). Magazine
operation:

loading to standby

The magazine is inserted and the
unit enters the "standby" mode as
described in the previous section.

(2). Disc loading operation:

When "Disc 3" is selected, the CPU
(Ic-401) energizes the elevation
motor to lift the disc loader. At
this time the elevator gear
generates two pulses per rotation.

The CPU monitors the rising edge of
the height sensor (D451) signal.

The disc loader elevates from one
disc to the next each time the CPU
detects a complete rotation. When
the CPU detects the target pulse
(Target - 1/2), it issues a command
to reduce the supply voltage to the
elevator motor (slow lift command).
At the target disc the CPU shuts off
the elevator motor current.

Disc tray "pull-out" sequence:

The loading motor is energized to
pull out the third disc tray. When
the CPU detects that the draw switch
(S453) is closed, it reduces the
supply voltage to the loading motor.
When the CPU subsequently detects
that the loading switch (S452) is
closed, it shuts off the loading
motor current.

. Disc clamp operation:

Since the optical deck is located
two disc span below the first disc,
the loading units must be lowered to
the C (clamp) position. To do this,
the CPU energizes the elevator motor
so that it will lower the disc
loader. This time, the CPU counts
the falling edge of the height
sensor pulses. When the CPU counts 8
pulses, it brakes the elevation
motor, and when it detects the
falling edge of the 9th pulse, it
stops the motor.

Play operation:

The disc clamper secures the disc on
the turntable and the optical deck
starts playing the disc.
