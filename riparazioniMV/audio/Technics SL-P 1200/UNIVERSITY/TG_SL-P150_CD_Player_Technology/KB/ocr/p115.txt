4. Height Adjustment of the Elevator
Unit

At rest (standby) position, the elevator
unit must be positioned 1.6mm above the
magazine holder surface.

The purpose of this adjustment is to set
this relative position. When performing
this adjustment, the shutter is adjusted
so that the pulse count (generated by the
photo detector and detected by the CPU)
corresponds to the proper height. If the
height is not properly set, the CPU dose
not detect the proper reference, causing
the elevator unit to either miss the
proper disc tray, access two disc trays,
or access the wrong disc tray.
