When IC-101 receives a Laser On (LD ON)
command (H,L,L,L), the logic controller
issues a corresponding command to the
internal APC block As a result, pin 5
of IC-101 is set High, which turns 0203,
and Q101 "on" and hence activates the LD
(laser diode)

A loop circuit is formed by Q101, LD (a
pair of the laser and PIN diode), and
APC of IC-101. APC is carried out by
detecting the quality of laser by the
PIN diode. If the beam of laser is
intensified, the resistance of the PIN
diode is lowered and the voltage at the

pin 10 of IC-101 is raised (the voltage
at the pin 12 of IC-101 is the refernce
voltage) to diminish the intensity of
the laser beam.

The APC requlates the LD's output power
by controlling the supply voltage
according to the output light intensity.
The LD's output light intensity is pre-
set at the factpory with the
potentiometer(VR) in the laser power
control circuit. Therefore do not adjust
this potentiometer as it requires
precision adjustment (or the left of the
LD may be shortened).
