a) Lead-in track:
In the lead-in track channel P is
encoded as music. The first piece of
music is preceded by a start flag of
2-3 seconds.

b) Lead-out track:

The lead-out track is preceded by a
start flag of 2-3 seconds (during the
last music track on the disc). The end
of the start flag gives the begin of
the lead-out track. The channel zero
for 2-3 seconds after the start of the
lead-out track, next P switches
between 0 and 1 ina 2Hz + 22 rhythm
(duty cycle 50+ 103).

The channel P can only change after the
subcoding sync pattern SO and Sl. The
encoding of channel P is one subcoding
block delayed to the encoding of channel
Q.

The general data format of channel Q is
as follows;

SO, Sl | CONTROL DATA-Q SO, Sl
