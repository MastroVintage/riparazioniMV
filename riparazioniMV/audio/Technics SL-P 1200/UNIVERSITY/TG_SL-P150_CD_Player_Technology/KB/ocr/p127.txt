C. Tracking Gain Adjustment

a) Purpose
During disc manufacturing, disc
eccentricity results due to tooling
tolerances. Eccentricity also results
if the tracking servo is designed to
overcome these tolerances and keep the
lens on track.

b)Principle of the tracking gain
adjustment

The tracking gain loop is opened at
which point a 1 KHz, 1.2 KHz, or 1.5
KHz signal is fed. This point is TP2
of the "Servo Gain Adjuster".

The signal, once through the servo,
appears at TP1l. The tracking gain
control is to be adjusted so that the
level of TP1l equals that of TP2, at
which point the gain is one (QdB)
