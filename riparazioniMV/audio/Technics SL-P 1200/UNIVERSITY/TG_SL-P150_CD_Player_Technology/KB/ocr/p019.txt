13. Error Correction Basics

1. Nature and Causes of Code Error

The error correction scheme iS an
essential part of the CD system. With
analog techniques, the effort of error
such as dropouts is that the sound is
merely skipped, and the listener may not

hear it, unless he is very attentive.
However, with digital audio, the
dropouts are heard in the form of

irritating pulse noise.

Roughly categorized, there are two types

of code error:

a) The first type are random error,
caused in disc production by
inaccurate photo-resist coating,
cutting, microscope bubbles, etc.

b) The second type are the burst error
resulting from scratches, dust and
fingerprints on the disc surface;
when these error occur, some bits or
even bytes may be missing, this means
dropouts are produced and this leads
to critical reabout error in the
digital data.
