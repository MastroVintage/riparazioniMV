Out of these 16,384 possible patterns,
the patterns are selected in which more
than two, but less than ten zeros appear
continuously (there must be at least two
"0" and no less than ten "0" between
each "1").

That means, only 267 patterns satisfy
this condition without 16,384 possible
patterns.

The 11 more patterns out of these 267
are elminated and the 256 remaining
combinations are used.

is used to assign the 256 combinations
(of the 8 bits code) to the 156
combinations (of the 14 bits code).

3. Junction Bits

To each block of 14 channel bits, 3
extra bits are added, 2 bits for
junction the blocks and 1 redundancy bit
for LF suppression.

contain any data, one of them can be
used to reduce the DC component in the
signal. To select the merging bits, the
