(4) 6T Brake Mode

The 6T brake mode is used to stop the
turntable rotation. The device enters
this mode when it receives a TTOFF
command from system IC-401 by MDATA pin
59.

The device first places its EC pin in
the forced deceleration mode. When the
minimum pulse width (6T) of the divide—
by-two SRF is reduced to approx, 7 times
that at the normal rotation rate, the
device sets the EC output to high
impedance and set the PC output high.

If a dropout occurs, the device does not
select brake release, so that the
turntable continues decelerating. A
dropout with a duration of less than 4.3
pis is however ignored, The figure 18-11
shows 6T brake mode.

(5) Speed Limiter

When the player uses a motor with FG,
the user can let the device control the
upper and lower limiter of motor speed
by applying the FG signal to the FG
input of the device.

When it is more than 77 Hz, the EC pin
is set high (forced deceleration).

If the FG generates 6 pulses’ per

rotation:

** EC provides forced acceleration at
140 rpm or less.

** EC provides forced deceleration at
770 rpm or more.
