21. CD Changer’s Operation

This section explains the SI,P405C CD
changer's mechanism operation's and
mechanism logic control operations
(including SL-P400C etc).

[Se 7

The SL-P405C incorporates a
recently-developed disc loading unit with
an elevating mechanism which enables the
user to load up to six compact discs in a
magazine and play them in the desired
sequence. The SL-P405C uses the One Beam
system (FF-1MK2) for its optical (laser)
pick-up.

The disc magazine allows loading and
unloading of individual discs. The disc
detection mechanism (provided only on
selected models) provides disc search and
displays the presence of discs on the
player's display.

The mechanism section uses three DC
motors for: Disc Loading, Elevator, and
the Spindle. These motors are controlled
by the system CPU (ICc-401).

The SL-P405Cc signal processing logic and
audio circuit are almost identical to
those used in our comparable CD players.

* The magazine holder is located at the
front-end of the mechanism. It holds
the magazine in place, containing six
discs (on trays), ready to be accessed
by the disc holder.

* The disc loader is located behind the
magazine holder. Its function is to
access the selected disc tray from the
magazine and lower it on the optical
deck's turntable.

* The optical deck is located below the
disc loader. Its function is to control
the optics used to read the disc's data
for Audio signal reproduction. The
optical deck is the heart of the CD
player. Its operation is explained on
page 31.

(1). Hook Catch:
The disc loader accesses a tray by
latching onto the HOOK CATCH of the
disc tray.

(2). Disc Sense Window:
This window is provided so that the
six disc sensing switches (provided
only on selected models) detect the
presence or absence of a disc.

(3). Disc Tray Lock:
The DISC TRAY LOCK retains the DISC
TRAYS in place so that they will not
drop out of the magazine
accidentally. When replacing a disc,
the user pushes this lock while
pulling the DISC TRAY.
