Tracking Servo Adjustment

1. Adjust the input level for the CD
player with the OSC level
controller @ on the unit panel

(Refer to the service manual for
the input level).

2. Select the frequency of the
oscillator with the frequency
switches (@ and @) on the unit
panel (Refer to the service
manual for the frequency).

3. Set the mode selector switch @ on
the unit panel to position "1" (for
tracking servo adjustment) and
adjust the semi fixed volume for
the tracking gain of the CD player
until the GOOD LED of the servo
gain indicator @ lights up.

NOTE

First adjust the semi fixed volume
for tracking gain control so that
the LOW LED on the servo gain
INDICATOR panel lights up and then
make further adjustments until the
GOOD LED lights up.
