8. Advantages of the CD Player

Table 8-1 is a comparison chart of the
LP and CD player system. It proves that
the CD player is far superior to the LP
player in every aspect of dynamic range,
distortion, frequency response, and wow
and flutter specifications. Especially,
the CD player exhibits a remarkably wide
90dB of dynamic range throughout the
entire audible frequency spectrum. In
contrast, the dynamic range of the LP
player is 70dB at its best.

In the low and high frequency range,
this figure becomes even worse and only
40 —- 50dB of dynamic range is reported.
Harmonic distortion of the CD system is
never worse than 0.01%, which is less
than one hundredth of that of the LP
player. In addition, wow and flutter are
simply too minute to be measured. This

The CD player also offers a number of
convenience features. Though manual
access to a piece of music on the disc
is simply impossible since the track
pitch is as fine as 1.6um, the CD player
enables total electronic control using
the built-in CPU and the eight control
bits inserted after the sync bits on the
disc. The control bits are equivalent to
approximately 2.7% of the total digital
codes recorded on the disc. Presently,
using two of these control bits, the
information described below can be
recorded for increased operating
convenience.
