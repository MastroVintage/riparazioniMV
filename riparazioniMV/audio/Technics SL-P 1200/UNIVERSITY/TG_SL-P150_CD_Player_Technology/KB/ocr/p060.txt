8) Traverse Servo Circuit

The 2-axis device can move horizintally
and thus follows a limited number of
tracks ( fine tracking ), Another servo
circuit, which called the traverse servo
circuit, is used to move the complete
optical unit across the disc surface

{ course tracking).

This servo circuit uses the = same
tracking error signal due to normal
playing mode (insted of the SKIP
mode only) as the feedback input, the
output of the tracking servo circuit is
linearly related to the tracking error
signal, whereas the output of the
traverse servo circuit has a built-in
hysteresis.

Only when the TE signal exceeds a fixed
threshold level, the traverse servo
drives the traverse linear motor. This
