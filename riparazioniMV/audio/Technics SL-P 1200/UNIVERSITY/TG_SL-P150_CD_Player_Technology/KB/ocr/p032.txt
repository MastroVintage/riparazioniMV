The light generated by recombination is
caught between the mirror side plates,
causes spontaneous emission, is
amplified and starts laser oscillation.
This stimulated emission can be so rapid
as to represent nearly an avalanche,
completely draining the high-energy
states in a subnanosecond burst of very
great energy.

On the other hand, certain types of
lasers can be produced which will
operate in an equilibrium condition,
giving up photons at just the input
energy pumping rate. The injection laser
diode operates in this mode.

The material in an injection laser dicde
is rather heavily doped so that, under
forward bias, The region near the
junction has a very high concentration
of holes an electrons. This produces the
inversion condition with a _ large
population of electrons in a high-energy
band and a large population of holes in
the low-energy band.

In this condition, the stimulated
emission of photons can overcome the
photon absorption and a net light flux
will result. In operation, the forward
current must reach some threshold, to
achieve lasing operation. Just beyond
this threshold, the Laser operates with
a single "thread" of light, and the
output is relatively stable but low.

As the current is increased, the light
output climbs extremely rapidly. The
light output is highly temperature
sensitive. A small current variation or
modest temperature change can cause the
output to rise so rapidly that it
destroys the chip.

A photodiode, monitoring the light
output, is commonly used in a feedback
loop to overcome this problem. Like the
LED, the ILD must be driven from a
current source, rather thana voltage
source as the unit can go into thermal
runaway.

As a device begins to draw current, the
junction begins to warm and forward drop
decreases, which tends to drive the
voltage higher and in turn decrease the
foeward drop until the current tends
toward infinity and the device is
destroyed.

In addition, the ILD has a tendency to
deterioration with operation, and this
deterioration is geratly accelerated
when operating the ILD outside of its
optimum limits. Therefore, in an optical
pickup assembly the operating point set
at the factory should not be touched
without the proper measuring equipment.
