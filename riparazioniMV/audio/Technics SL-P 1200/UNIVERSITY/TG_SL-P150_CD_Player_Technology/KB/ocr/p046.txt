17. Servo Circuit

The servo IC AN8370S must satisfy the
proper control needs for the optical
pickup functions. In addition, it must
also include the following functions,
which are not directly related to the
optical servo circuit functions.

1) Focus servo pull-in
2) Trick play
3) Laser power control

Before we begin a description of the
servo circuit operation, let's take a
look at the servo IC (AN8370S) which
integrates all the related
servo functions (see the block diagram
figure 17-1). And the servo IC contains
the following functional blocks:

1) Laser power control

2) RF amplifier

3) Focus servo head amplifier

4) Focus servo pull-in logic

5) Tracking servo head amplifier
6) system logic control

7) Trick play logic

8) Traverse speed control

***The focus servo pull-in logic is a
completely local function of the
servo IC and can drastically reduce
the load on the system controller
CPU.

***The tracking servo head amplifier,
which is based on the digital accu-
servo system is comprised of high-
speed ECL devices, and is capable of
detecting phase differences of
approx, 20 through 30 ns.

***The trick play logic controls the
high-speed access operation
characteristic of the CD player. It
consists of a KICK pulse generator,
tracking actuator holding circuit,
servo pull-in stabilizer, cross-track

count detector, and some other
related logic.
***The traverse speed controller

regulates the optical pickup track
crossing speed. It is compatible with
high-speed traverse mechanism that
use a linear motor( most CD player
used linear motor operation).
