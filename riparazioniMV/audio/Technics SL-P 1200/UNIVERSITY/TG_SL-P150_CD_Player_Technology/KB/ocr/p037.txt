3. Optical Pickup Construction and
Operation

Theoptical pickup is the heart of the CD
player. It pickup modulated laser beam
bounced of the disc pits and converts it
into electrical data. The pickup
(including the traverse mechanism)
requires special precision design. On
the Technics CD player series, the
pickup is integrated onto a_ single
assemly board and is mechanically
isolated from the player chassis by a
suspention mechanism to prevent floor
viarations and acoustic feedback fron
the loudspeakers.
