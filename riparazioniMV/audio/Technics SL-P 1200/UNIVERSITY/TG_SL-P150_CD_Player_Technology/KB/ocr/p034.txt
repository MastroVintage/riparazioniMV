b) Absorptance

Some of the optical radiation
impinging upon any substance is
absorbed by the substance.
Absorptance varies according to the
properties of the substance and the
wavelength and in real circumstances
may range from less than 1% to more
than 98% ("lampblack" body).

d) Refraction

A ray of optical radiation passing
from one medium to another is bent at
the interface of the two mediums if
the angle of incidence is 90 degree.
The index of refraction is the sine
of the angle of incidence divided by
the sine of the angle of refraction.
Refractive index varies with
wavelength and ranges from 1.0003 to
2.7.
