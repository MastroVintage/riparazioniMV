5) Need for Filtering

With 16 bits (65536 possible numbers)
and a sampling frequency of 44.1KHz, the
CD system is theoretically capable of
far higher reproduction quality than any
conventional record player. But to
achieve this quality, the output signal
that the player derives from the disc
must contain only the original audio
frequencies, and not the periodic
repetitions of the audio frequencies
produced by the sampling (see figure 19-
2).

These frequency bands can overload the
power amplifier, and mix with audio
frequencies or other signals to cause
audible interference. They must be
filtered out.
