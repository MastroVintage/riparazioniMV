D. Tracking offset adjustment

Perform the same procedure as for focus offset except use the TE testpoint.
This adjustment allows the tracking servo to compensate for horizontal

control of the focus servo. Adjust the tracking offset pot so that the dip(s) is

(are) minimized or eliminated. The TE waveform must be adjusted so that A
and B are balanced.
