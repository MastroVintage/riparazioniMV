Procedure
Step1. Clean the lens and guide rails

Use the cleaning (Part Number SZZP1038C) to clean the lens. Moisten a cotton
rod with cleaning solution and wipe the optic lens from center to edge. Clean the
guide rails by using a lint-free cloth to remove any visible dirt or dust. Lubricate
the guide rails by applying the prescribed traverse oil (see page 8).

Step 2. Observe RF waveform

Connect an oscilloscope to the RF test point (refer to S/M for specific location)
and play track 8 of the playability test disc CDT-016 (Part Number SZZP1054C).
Observe the waveform and compare it to the minimum peak to peak voltage
allowed for it (see page 8). If the observed waveform is low, it is first necessary to
perform the next step BEFORE replacing the optical pickup unit.
