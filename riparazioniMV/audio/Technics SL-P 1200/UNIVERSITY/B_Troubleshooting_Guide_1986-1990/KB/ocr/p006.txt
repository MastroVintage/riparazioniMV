Step 5.

Electrical adjustments (continued)

RB. Tracking gain adjustment

Change the mode select switch on the servo gain adjuster to position “2” and
select the correct oscillator frequency. Adjust the tracking gain variable
resistor so that the amplitudes at TP1 and TP2 are equal while playing track 8

of the playability test disc. This adjustment makes the gain of the tracking
servo equal to 1.

C. Focus offset adjustment

Connect channels 1 and 2 of the oscilloscope to the RF and TE test points on
the CD player’s PCB. Playback the black band test disc (Part Number
SZZP1057C). This will simulate manufacturing defects on CDs and looks like
a “dip” on the RF waveform. Adjust the focus offset pot so that the dip is

minimized or eliminated. The FE waveform must be adjusted so that A and B
are at minimum or balanced.
