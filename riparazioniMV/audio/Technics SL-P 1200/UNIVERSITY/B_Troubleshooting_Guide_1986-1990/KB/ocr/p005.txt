Step 4. Improve playability
A. Test focus and tracking servo circuits

Playing the 0.9mm wedge (track 8) on the playability test disc serves to show
how well the unit is at tracking defects on discs. If skipping occurs, first

inspect the test disc for scratches or dust. If skipping persists, it may be
necessary to perform the mechanical adjustment over again (See Step 3C).

B. Perform PLL adjustment

Play the 0.8mm wedge on the playability test disc and connect the
oscilloscope to the PLL test point. Adjust the PLL variable resistor so its
position is between the two locations where the PLL waveform starts to be
disturbed. This adjustment is used to set the operating range of the voltage
controlled oscillator that allows the disc to be synchronized with the
mechanism’s internal clock.

Step 5. Electrical adjustments
A. Focus gain adjustment

Conncect the servo gain adjuster (Part Number SZZP1017o0r SZZP1094C) as
explained in the Service Manual for the model being serviced. Connect
channels 1 and 2 of the oscilloscope to TP1 and TP2 on the servo gain
adjuster. Play track 1 of the playability test disc and turn the focus gain
adjustment pot so that the wave forms at TP1 and TP2 are of equal amplitude.
This is done to set the focus gain to unity, thereby stabilizing the lens focus.
