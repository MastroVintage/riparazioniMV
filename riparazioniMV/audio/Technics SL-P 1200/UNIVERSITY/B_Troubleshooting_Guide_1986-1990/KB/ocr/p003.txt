B. Adjust turntable height

The disc may be beyond the operating range of the focus servo. If so, itis
necessary to position the turntable to the physical midpoint of the servo’s
operating range. Play track 1 of the playability test disc and connect the
oscilloscope to the focus offset test point. Adjust the turntable height so as to
approach 0 volts as close as possible. This adjustment is a must when
mechanical parts in the traverse deck are replaced or removed
